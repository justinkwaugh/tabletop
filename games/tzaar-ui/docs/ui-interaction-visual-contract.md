# UI Interaction Visual Contract

## 1. Visual Intents

| Intent        | Trigger                                                                   | What should be emphasized                                                                                                                                                                                                      | What should be dimmed                | What must remain unaffected |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| place_piece   | The viewer is placing (tournament, or setting an I set, you choose board) | The chosen type among the action panel's type buttons; on the board nothing, since every empty space takes a piece; type buttons in the tone and counts of the colour going down, which the banner names when setting for both | Types the viewer has none left of    | Pieces on the board         |
| choose_color  | The viewer takes a colour (I set, you choose)                             | White and Black buttons in the action panel; White opens                                                                                                                                                                       | Nothing                              | The board                   |
| choose_stack  | The viewer may capture or stack                                           | Every stack with a legal capture or stacking (a cyan ring on the flat edge of its top piece)                                                                                                                                   | Nothing                              | Other stacks                |
| choose_target | A stack is chosen                                                         | The chosen stack (thicker ring), a dashed cyan ring, each dash outlined in black, on every stack it can capture and every own stack it may be stacked onto                                                                     | Other movable stacks' marks (hidden) | Other stacks                |
| pass          | The viewer is on the second move                                          | A Pass button in the action panel while no stack is chosen                                                                                                                                                                     | Nothing                              | The board                   |

## 2. Visual Primitives

- **Stack** (`StackShape.svelte`, sizes in `board/pieceGeometry.ts`, shape in `board/pieceShape.ts`, colors and specks in `board/pieceStyle.ts`, speck paths in `board/specks.ts`): MATRX GIPF's discs seen from the near side of the table, each resting a little above the one below with a dark crease between; only the top piece shows its top: a flat edge, a groove whose lips round down into a level floor, a flat ring and a sunken centre with a rounded edge. A Tzarra's centre is metal, and a Tzaar's groove too: gold on white, silver on black. Each whole piece is an image of its tone and type, pre-rendered by `scripts/render-looks.mjs` from the `/looks` page at 6.25 px per board unit (the board's largest fit, 2.5, at 2.5 device pixels per CSS pixel) (`DiscLook.svelte`, `LookDefs.svelte`; rerun it after changing how a piece is drawn), with its own specks drawn over it as one path per colour, clipped to the piece (`PieceDefs.svelte`); a thinned piece is a shaded band with its specks fitted inside it. With the Stack limit on, past five pieces (nine on the rotated board, `ROTATED_PIECE_MODEL`, where the nearest pieces behind stand half a piece to either side) a stack rises no higher, so a single piece behind it keeps about 44% (54%) showing, and the pieces between its bottom and top thin to bands. The Stack limit toggle (`shrinkStacks` title preference, off by default) thins tall stacks as above when on; off, every stack stands whole, and players turn the board to see past a tall one; the frame then grows over any stack that would stand out of it, and over a stack in flight at its highest (`BoardView.raiseFor`, eased by the animator on the step's timeline; see §4), so the board shrinks to keep it in view; it keeps room above the far row for whole stacks four pieces taller than the limit (`WHOLE_HEADROOM`: 9 on the board, 13 rotated), so it grows less often, at the cost of a board about 5% smaller while stacks are left whole, since the board is fitted by its height on most screens. A stack of two or more carries its count on a small white badge, 0.27 of the piece's radius (11px across on a 390px-wide phone, larger than the other titles' 9px to suit TZAAR's larger pieces), at the far right of its top piece as in DVONN, clear of the type's markings; on the far left if a nearer stack covers that, and at the top's far middle if it covers both (`board/badgeSpot.ts`, chosen in `Board.svelte` from the stacks drawn after it; the digits set ten times larger and scaled down, since text that small is snapped off-centre on phones, on the alphabetic baseline lowered by 0.36 of their size in user units). A stack in flight carries its badge at the far right. The Height badges toggle (`heightBadges` title preference, on by default; the chip after A1, the overlay's chips running A1, Height badges, Stack limit, 90°, Far side) hides them, except on stacks shrunk past the full height, whose height cannot be seen. Speckled like the real pieces on every plastic surface at one density per area, seeded by each piece's id, spread evenly with no bare patches (best-candidate placement): on the side evenly round its curve; on the top out to the edge, each speck lit as the surface under it, shrunk to keep off the lips and the edge (left out if it would shrink too far), and left off the metal and wherever the top nearer you hides it. Never carries interaction state.
- **Shadows** (`Board.svelte`): a radial-gradient ellipse under each stack, inside the stack's animated group, so it moves and fades with it, and the board in shade right under it, showing through the gap below the bottom piece. No filters on pieces.
- **Board** (`Board.svelte`, outline and lines from `board/geometry.ts`; the `rotatedBoard` title preference, toggled by the 90° overlay button, turns it a quarter turn so its flat sides face the players, columns running across with A at the top and labels beside both ends of each row; the `turned` title preference, toggled by the two-arrow Far side button beside it, turns it half a turn, alone or with the quarter turn, so what tall stacks hide comes in front, labels staying outside the board; the flecks on each top turn with the board; every overlay toggle's hover text, its `hint`, says what a press does now): the printed board's pale play area, a hexagon less the one round the missing centre, on the golden table; black lines along the three directions between neighbouring spaces, never across the centre, running a quarter step past the board's edge.
- **Marks** (`Board.svelte`, passed to `StackShape` as its `onTop` snippet and drawn on the top piece under the count badge, so nearer stacks cover them as they cover pieces, the badge stays whole, and a captured stack's mark rises and fades with it; the animator fades every mark with the move): two-tone ellipses, a dark band under a cyan core (`--tzaar-mark`), on the flat edge of the top piece at the stack's current height, so a mark never reaches a neighbour. Inputs `session.movable`, `session.chosenFrom`, `session.captureTargets`, `session.stackTargets`.
- **Coordinate labels** (`board/coordinates.ts`, placed by `BoardView.labelAt`): the rulebook's names for the ends of each column, the top one a gap above a single piece on that space and the bottom one a gap below its near edge, on the table; on the alphabetic baseline lowered by 0.35 of their size in user units, with letter-spacing in px, which every browser draws alike. A taller stack on a top space covers its label.
- **Action panel height** (`ActionPanel.svelte`, `components/holdTallest.ts`): the panel keeps the tallest height it has reached at its current width, so a line that wraps for a long name does not move the board when it shrinks back. A new width starts over; a remount (the end panel, a new game) starts over too.

## 4. Shared State Contracts

- **Animation scope**: the board animates only `own-action`, `other-action`, `undo`, `history` and `exploration` transitions; page loads, server syncs, edits and view switches show it at rest. All motion is on a timeline added to the step's `actionTimeline`, so the presenter waits for it; it starts once the step's stacks are drawn, waiting at most 0.01 s.
- **Reading a step**: every step reads the two boards by piece id (`boardDiff`). Pieces that leave one space together for another are one run, drawn as one flier and flown straight from where it stood to where it ends, so a history jump over several moves flies each run start to end. A stack whose pieces all leave the board vanishes and one whose pieces are all new appears. No action or move is needed to read a step, so Undo, another player's Undo and history steps read alike.
- **A step with an action** plays in full, history replay included: a stack hops in one arc (0.32 s, 0.04 s longer per space up to 0.52 s, 22 units high and 3 more per space), rebounding along the hop when it lands on the board and bouncing mostly up and down when it lands on a stack; a captured stack floats up 34 units and fades over 0.24 s as the stack hops in; a placed piece falls from 50 units over 0.26 s and settles with a small wobble. With the Stack limit on, a stack grown past it is drawn whole once the landing settles, at the height the two stood (each part at most the limit, so it does not jump as it lands), and shrinks a piece at a time to the limit over 0.3 s, stepped in timeline callbacks. Marks fade over 0.2 s.
- **A step without an action** (Undo, another player's Undo, a state-only history step, entering or leaving an exploration) ends within 0.18 s, under the 0.2 s budget, without bounces: a run hops in 0.18 s; going forward a captured stack floats off over 0.11 s and a placed piece drops in over 0.18 s; going back a captured stack drops back in from 0.06 s to 0.18 s while the stack that took it hops away, and a placed piece lifts off over 0.18 s. A stack past the limit is drawn at the limit when the state lands. Marks fade over 0.18 s. A step that changes nothing on the board adds nothing to the timeline.
- **The frame** (`animator.raise`): with stacks left whole, a step eases it up over whatever stands out of it during the step (the stacks drawn, the stacks it lands, and each flier at its highest) from the start, over 0.3 s or half the step, whichever is shorter, and down to what the landed board needs over the same length at the step's end. Otherwise, and on page loads, silent swaps and view switches, it is set at once from the board shown.
- **A lone flier** passes behind the stacks nearer the viewer than its place on the board while it is lower than their tops (`<use>` copies of those stacks drawn over it, placed by a clock tweened with the hop, so a seek places them too); with several fliers none is occluded.
- **Shared animator state**: `stacks` (a writable derivation of the shown board; a step writes the board partway, and its end writes the landed board only while the state it started from is still the one shown), `fliers`, `raise`, and the stack shrinking with its height so far. All are put back when the step's animations end.
- **Interruption** (`session.moveInterrupters`): toggling Rotated board, Far side or Stack limit lands the move at once, its motion having been laid out in the old view; a switch of view lands it and drops the rest of its replay. All motion is `translate()`/`scale()` through `animators/svgPose.ts`, never GSAP's `matrix()`, which Firefox 140 misplaces under the board's zoom.
- **Staged selection** (`selection.from`, a space key from the logic's `spaceKey`): manual choices only, registered with `localSelections`, so Undo lets go of the chosen stack before it undoes the last committed action; the header's Undo shows while `hasLocalSelection` or an undoable action. Tapping the chosen stack again, or a tap on the board that hits nothing choosable (`session.letGo()`, from a `role="presentation"` wrapper round the board), also lets go of it. The placing type is not part of it. The selection belongs to the player who made it: anyone else viewing (the harness's Non-active view) sees none of it, and their Undo passes it by. Taps on the board and Pass do nothing while a move plays; the type buttons stay usable.
- **Restaging**: your own Undo of a capture or stacking hops the stack home and leaves it chosen (`beforeNewState` on `undo`), checked on the state being shown (`restagedMove`: only the move whose index is that state's action count, and only while the view stays with its maker). The next Undo lets go of it. Another player's Undo, a history step and every other transition choose nothing; the move being undone is forgotten once any state lands.
- **Target lists**: derived from the displayed state; empty when the viewer cannot act, including in History View.

## 5. Forbidden Couplings

- Do not derive legality in components; read the session lists.

## 6. Interaction Matrix

| Intent A      | Intent B      | Can coexist? | Winner / combination rule                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| choose_stack  | choose_target | yes          | A tap on a target captures or stacks; a tap on another movable stack that is not a target chooses that one; the chosen stack lets go |
| choose_target | pass          | no           | Pass shows only while no stack is chosen                                                                                             |

## 7. Regression Checklist

- Choose a stack: thicker ring on it, dashed rings on every capture and stacking target; tap it again, or tap off it on the board, to let go.
- Two Tzaars left: capture D1 onto D2; the captured Tzaar floats up as the stack lands; capture F1 onto F2 and the game ends.
- Covering a type: after capturing A3 onto A5, only A1 onto A2 or B1 and A5 onto A2 are offered.
- Undo after a capture: the stack hops back and stays chosen while the captured stack drops back in, all within 0.2 s; every stack group has opacity 1 and no transform afterwards.
- Tall stacks with the Stack limit on: stacking D2 onto D3 lands at the height the two stood, then shrinks to 5 pieces; nothing jumps as it lands.
- Tall stack on E8 with the limit off: each move eases the frame up over the tall stack; Undo eases it back within the step.
- Toggle Rotated board, Far side or Stack limit mid-move: the move lands at once in the new view.
- History jump to the start or end over a capture and a stacking with the same stack: it flies straight start to end, the captured stack lifting off or dropping back.
- Refresh: two screenshots 0.1 s and 1 s after opening are identical.
- History View: no marks or targets.

## 8. Change Rules

When touching interactive visuals:

1. identify the intent being changed
2. identify the primitives involved
3. verify whether shared state contracts are being widened
4. update this document if a new interaction or coupling is introduced
