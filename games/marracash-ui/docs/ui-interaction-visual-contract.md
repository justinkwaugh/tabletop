Covers the board highlights and previews used while a player takes their turn. Selections live in `MarracashGameSession`; the board only renders them.

### Touch screens

On a screen without hover (`usesTouch` in the session, from the `(hover: none)` media query), hover previews become taps:

- Tapping a destination fountain stages it as `destination` and shows its route preview instead of moving; the action panel asks "Move these visitors along the highlighted route?" with **Move here** and Back. Back clears the destination first, then the fountain. Desktop keeps hover to preview and click to move, and never shows **Move here**.
- Tapping a history entry or a customer count toggles its board highlight; tapping it again, or another one, replaces or clears it. Pointer hover is ignored for touch input. Both are also buttons that Enter or Space toggles.
- Every selectable fountain is tappable across its whole cell. When a contain fit would draw the board below 45% scale, as on phones and portrait tablets, the board rests at a cover fit and pans; the zoom buttons sit at its top-left, over mid-queue pawns, with that strip kept clear.
- On narrow screens the money report leaves out the bids line, which History keeps.

## Coexistence and precedence

| Intent A             | Intent B             | Rule                                                                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Choose a fountain    | Choose a shop        | Coexist until a fountain is selected. Selecting a fountain removes the shop highlights, because an auction can't follow choosing a move.                                          |
| Choose a destination | Choose a fountain    | Exclusive: selecting a fountain removes the other movable fountains' halos under the overlay. Back returns to choosing an action, where any fountain or shop can be chosen.       |
| Preview a route      | Choose a destination | The preview only exists while its route starts at the selected fountain.                                                                                                          |
| Visitors walk        | Choose a destination | Exclusive: choices are withheld while the session is busy, so the overlay, halos and preview are gone before the first pawn leaves and nothing can be chosen until the walk ends. |

During bidding, while waiting for others, and in History View, the session reports no available actions, so no intent shows. The auction spotlight comes from game state, not available actions, so it shows to every player during bidding, including in History View.

## Shared visual state

- **Staged selection** (`fountain`, `shop`, `queueEnd`, `visitorCount` in the session):
    - Meaning: the player's manual choices before an action is committed.
    - Producers and consumers: board clicks and the refill panel set it. Board highlights and the panels read it.
    - Lifetime: cleared by `resetAction()` before each new state is published. Back and Undo remove the latest manual choice.
    - Validity: each derived value is undefined unless its action is currently available, so stale choices never render.
    - Eligibility: while the session is busy (an action is being applied or a state change animates), staged values derive as undefined, no fountain, shop, entrance or queue pawn is offered, and the staging methods ignore input, so nothing chosen mid-transition is silently cleared by `resetAction()`.
    - A single legal visitor count is derived, never stored, so Back and Undo never consume it.
- **Hovered route** (`Board`):
    - Meaning: the route to the destination fountain under the pointer or focus.
    - Producer: the destination `FountainSpot`s. Consumer: `RoutePreview`.
    - Lifetime: cleared on pointer leave or blur, and reset whenever the selected fountain changes or a new state starts publishing, so choosing a destination (whose spot unmounts without a pointer leave) never leaves a stale preview.
    - Validity: derived empty while a new state is publishing, or when the route doesn't start at the selected fountain.

- **Final round banner** (`finalTurnPlayerId` in the session):
    - Meaning: the queue has run out, so the game ends after the last seat in turn order finishes this round.
    - Producer: derived from `finalRound`, `result` and the turn order. Consumer: `FinalRoundBanner`, always first in the action panel.
    - Lifetime: from the moment the queue empties until the game ends; it names the last player ("The game ends after Bashir's turn."), or says "This is the last turn of the game." during that player's turn.

- **Money report** (`moneyReports` in the session):
    - Meaning: every payment made by the latest player action and the system actions that followed it (an auction resolving, an antique set paying out), one row per payment (an auction's walk-ins from several fountains share one "walked in" row), plus the bids and any tie winner for an auction. Never running totals.
    - Producer: derived from the visible actions by `moneyReport.ts`. Consumers: `MoneyReport` at the top of the action panel; the history reuses `AuctionBids`.
    - Lifetime: hidden while a shop is staged for auction, and replaced by the next player action; a step that moves no money, such as a sealed bid or a move into no owned shop, shows nothing. Antique sets a turn completed appear only with the step that commits the turn: Confirm turn, the last entrance placement, or starting an auction.
    - Validity: derived from the visible context, so History View shows the report for the step being viewed. Payments are public under Concealed Cash (see the title's rulings), so every player sees the same report.

- **Visitor move presentation** (`movingVisitors`, `fountainVisitorOverrides`, `shopCustomerOverrides` in the session):
    - Meaning: the pawns in motion during a move, an undo or a history step, and the fountain crowds and shop customer counts as they stand mid-animation.
    - Producer: `VisitorMoveAnimator`. Consumers: `Board` draws the moving pawns and renders fountains and shops from the session's `visibleFountains` and `visibleShops`.
    - Lifetime: the moving pawns last for one animated action and are cleared after its animations; the count overrides last until `beforeNewState()` clears them as the new state publishes, so counts never fall back to the old state between the animation and the publish.

- **History highlight** (`historyHighlight` in the session):
    - Meaning: where the History entry under the pointer took place.
    - Producer: `History` entries on pointer enter and leave. Consumer: `HistoryHighlight` on the board.
    - Lifetime: cleared when the pointer leaves the entry or the History tab unmounts.

- **Customer highlight** (`customerHighlight` in the session):
    - Meaning: the player and market colour whose customer count is under the pointer.
    - Producer: `PlayerState`'s counter slots on pointer enter and leave. Consumer: `ShopTile`, through `ShopSign`, haloes the matching `PawnCountChip`s.
    - Lifetime: cleared when the pointer leaves the slot or the Players tab unmounts.

- The bid panel's − and + step 25 Dirham per press; held down, they repeat after 0.5 s and speed up after eight steps, stopping on release, on leaving the button, or at the minimum bid or the player's cash. Keyboard activation steps once.
- Action panel buttons darken slightly on hover: filled buttons to a deeper brown, outlined ones with a light brown tint, and a selected refill choice like a filled button.

- **Short-queue warning** (`showQueueTooShort` in the session):
    - Meaning: the last queue pawn clicked can't be read as one end and count.
    - Producer: `VisitorQueue`. Consumer: `RefillPanel`.
    - Lifetime: a few seconds, or until a pawn choice is made, a new state publishes, or refilling ends.

## Render ownership

- `Board` owns the layer order: the visitor queue in the margin outside the walls (drawn last instead, above the overlay, while entrances are being refilled), then shops, then fountains, then the shared overlay, then whatever is lifted above it (the auctioned or staged shop, the shops the previewed route enters, the selected fountain and its destinations, or the empty entrances awaiting a refill), then the route preview and any history highlight. Pawns moving during a move animation are drawn on a separate transparent layer above the whole board, its own composited layer, so their motion never repaints the filter-heavy board. The overlay blocks clicks on everything beneath it, so only lifted pieces are interactive; the preview and the moving pawns ignore pointer events so they never block a destination.
- History entries are one short sentence; a refill shows the pawns it brought, in the order they entered, as one unbreakable group of pawn icons: shops are named by colour in the market colour ("put a yellow shop up for auction", "bought the yellow shop"), never by ID. Payments appear below as rows built from the money report's payments: player tag, a signed amount in green (+) or red (−), then pawns of the customers' colour, an arrow to the player receiving a mover's cut, or "cut" for an auctioneer's cut. An auction's winning bid is left out since the sentence gives the price. Hidden text gives screen readers the meaning of each row.
- Players never see fountain numbers: fountains carry no number labels, and the money report and history describe moves, refills and walk-ins without naming fountains ("You moved 6 visitors."). Screen-reader labels keep the numbers so each fountain stays distinguishable.
- `ShopTile` draws each owned shop's `ShopSign`: a flat cardboard sign in the owner's colour, cut in a shape unique to their seat, with the customer count beside it. That count and the route preview's entering counts use the same `PawnCountChip`: a chip with a pawn and a number on or beside a shop always means visitors of that colour in the shop. Crowded fountains use their own tally panel instead.
- `GameEndPanel` gives the winner line in bold, then one finisher per line as a three-column list: rank, name tag right-aligned, cash left-aligned. Equal cash shares a rank and the next rank skips (1, 1, 3); tied players are listed in seating order.
- `PlayerState` reads, top to bottom: name and cash; "Customers:" with "Shops: n/6" on the right; the customer counter, a pawn and count per market colour in fixed two-digit slots so it never changes width, with colours at zero faded; then the antiques line ("Antiques: n/5" over the viewer's own cards).
- Each antique card shows its colour's item (yellow signet ring, red brass lantern, blue teapot, purple perfume bottle, green curved dagger) above its value. Every card has a border in the vivid market colour. In the owner's hand an item is a white figure on a near-white card until a customer of that colour covers the card; then the item fills with the market colour and the card takes the colour's pale tint. Hands and revealed sets are shown sorted by market colour in the customer counter's order (red, blue, green, purple, yellow), then by value. Clicking anywhere on the owner's hand toggles a line below it listing, in that order with counts, the colours still missing a customer ("Missing: Red, 2x Purple"), or "Set complete" once every card is covered but the set is not yet paid. The owner's revealed set toggles its finishing order instead ("First completed set"). It is local to that player's panel, and only the viewer's own hand is ever drawn, so it reveals nothing about other players' cards.
- `PlayerState` shows a revealed antique set face up to every player, with its award position; cards outside the paid best cards for that position show their items in colour and are dimmed, using the logic's `paidAntiques`.

## Verification scenarios

All checked manually in the dev harness, using scripted browser runs.

1. **Round 2 turn:**
    - At the start, both fountains with visitors and unowned shops are highlighted.
    - Selecting fountain 1 dims the table and leaves only fountain 1 and its destinations 2, 3 and 6 bright; the panel asks for the destination.
    - Hovering fountain 6 previews the route to it.
    - Back, or clicking fountain 1 again, restores the start-of-turn highlights.
    - Hovering fountain 4 from fountain 8 lifts the yellow and purple shops its visitors would enter, each with a chip counting one visitor.
    - Clicking fountain 6 moves the visitors there.
2. **Entrance refill:**
    - After two moves that empty entrances 1 and 8, the board dims and both empty entrances are highlighted but not yet clickable; the queue stays bright.
    - Choosing the front of the queue and 3 visitors makes both entrances clickable and haloes the three incoming pawns; clicking the fourth pawn from the back instead selects the back and 4.
    - With 5 or 6 visitors waiting, clicking a middle pawn shows the short-queue warning in the refill panel and changes nothing.
    - Clicking entrance 1 places the visitors and clears the selection.
    - The second refill starts from a fresh selection.
3. **Auction:** clicking an unowned shop dims the table around it, haloes it and asks for confirmation; Back restores the turn. Start auction starts bidding, and the spotlight stays until the auction resolves.
4. **Money report:**
    - The first auction of a game, with every bid at 100, reports the auctioneer's winning bid, lists all four bids and says the auctioneer wins the tie; the history entry gives the reason.
    - A move into two owned shops shows each owner's customers and each mover's cut as separate rows, the cut pointing to the mover.
    - The next player action, such as starting the next auction, clears the report.
5. **Visitor move animation:**
    - From fountain 3 holding blue, red, purple, green and two yellow pawns, with blue, purple, red and yellow shops owned along its routes, moving west to fountain 4 clears the highlights, then walks the pawns single file; purple, red and both yellows turn into their shops, whose chips count up, and blue and green join fountain 4 as fountain 3 empties one pawn at a time.
    - The south and north moves from the same setup branch only the matching shops along those routes.
6. **Undo a move:** Undo straight after that move glides every pawn back to fountain 3 in 200ms, out of the shops and fountain 4, and restores the earlier counts.
