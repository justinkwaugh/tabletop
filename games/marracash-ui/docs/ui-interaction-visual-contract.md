# MarraCash UI interaction visual contract

Covers the board highlights and previews used while a player takes their turn. Selections live in `MarracashGameSession`; the board only renders them.

### Touch screens

A finger has no hover, so hover highlights become taps. Each tap is judged by its own pointer type, so a touchscreen laptop gets taps from a finger and hover from its mouse.

- Tapping a history entry or a customer count toggles its board highlight; tapping it again, or another one, replaces or clears it. Pointer hover is ignored for touch input. Both are also buttons that Enter or Space toggles. The shared `hoverOrTap` action owns this hover, tap and key handling.
- Every selectable fountain is tappable across its whole cell. When a contain fit would draw the board below 45% scale, as on phones and portrait tablets, the board rests at a cover fit and pans; the zoom buttons sit at its top-left, over mid-queue pawns, with that strip kept clear.

## Coexistence and precedence

| Intent A               | Intent B                                                        | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Choose a fountain      | Choose a shop                                                   | Coexist until a fountain is selected. Selecting a fountain removes the shop highlights, because an auction can't follow choosing a move.                                                        |
| Choose a destination   | Choose a fountain                                               | Exclusive: selecting a fountain removes the other movable fountains' halos under the overlay. Undo returns to choosing an action, where any fountain or shop can be chosen.                     |
| Preview a route        | Choose a destination                                            | The preview only exists while its route starts at the selected fountain.                                                                                                                        |
| Visitors walk          | Choose a destination                                            | Exclusive: choices are withheld while the session is busy, so the overlay, halos and preview are gone before the first pawn leaves and nothing can be chosen until the walk ends.               |
| Visitors walk          | History navigation                                              | History controls ignore input while an action is being applied or a history step is animating, so a walk always finishes and the view settles on the moved state; later steps start from there. |
| Trace a customer count | Spotlight the auction, Choose a destination, Choose an entrance | Coexist: every matching chip keeps its halo. Chips sit on the shop signs, which stay above the overlay, so every haloed chip shows at full strength.                                            |

During bidding, while waiting for others, and in History View, the session reports no available actions, so no intent shows and any staged choice is hidden. The auction spotlight comes from game state, not available actions, so it shows to every player during bidding, including in History View. A history step lands after a player action and before the system actions it triggers, so the step after an auction's last sealed bid shows the spotlight with "All bids are in." in the action panel.

## Shared visual state

- **Staged selection** (`fountain`, `destination`, `shop`, `refill` in the session):
    - Meaning: the player's manual choices before an action is committed. Only the moving fountain and the refill choice are staged; a destination or a shop acts at once.
    - Producers and consumers: board clicks and taps and the refill panel set it. Board highlights and the panels read it.
    - Lifetime: cleared by `resetAction()` before each new state is published, including each History View step. Undo removes the latest staged entry. `refill` holds the queue end and count together as one entry, so a queue pawn, which sets both, is undone by one Undo.
    - Validity: each derived value is undefined unless its action is currently available, so stale choices never render.
    - Eligibility: while the session is busy (an action is being applied or a state change animates), staged values derive as undefined, no fountain, shop, entrance or queue pawn is offered, and the staging methods ignore input, so nothing chosen mid-transition is silently cleared by `resetAction()`.
    - A single legal visitor count is derived, never stored, so Undo never consumes it.
- **Hovered route** (`Board`):
    - Meaning: the route to the destination fountain under the pointer or focus.
    - Producer: the destination `FountainSpot`s. Consumer: `RoutePreview`.
    - Lifetime: cleared on pointer leave or blur, and reset whenever the selected fountain changes or a new state starts publishing, so choosing a destination (whose spot unmounts without a pointer leave) never leaves a stale preview.
    - Validity: derived empty while a new state is publishing, or when the route doesn't start at the selected fountain.

- **Final round banner** (`finalTurnPlayerId` in the session):
    - Meaning: the queue has run out, so the game ends after the last seat in turn order finishes this round.
    - Producer: the game state's `finalTurnPlayerId()`, from `finalRound`, the end-of-game state and the turn order. Consumer: `FinalRoundBanner`, always first in the action panel.
    - Lifetime: from the moment the queue empties until the game ends; it names the last player ("The game ends after Bashir's turn."), or says "This is the last turn of the game." during that player's turn.

- **Outcome line** (`outcomes` in the session):
    - Meaning: who won a shop at auction, or completed an antique set, in the latest player action and the system actions that followed it. The action panel shows only instructions to the current player, led by this one short sentence ("Bashir won the red shop.", "Amira completed their antique set.") on the same line as the prompt. Payments, bids and move details are left to History.
    - Producer: derived from the visible actions by `moneyReport.ts` (`isOutcomeReport`). Consumer: `Outcomes`, rendered at the start of the active panel's prompt.
    - Lifetime: replaced by the next player action. An antique set appears with the step that ends the turn or starts an auction.
    - Validity: derived from the visible context, so History View shows the outcome of the step being viewed.
- **Visitor move presentation** (`movingVisitors`, `fountainVisitorOverrides`, `shopCustomerOverrides` in the session):
    - Meaning: the pawns in motion during a move, an undo or a history step, and the fountain crowds and shop customer counts as they stand mid-animation.
    - Producer: `VisitorMoveAnimator`. Consumers: `Board` draws the moving pawns and renders fountains and shops from the session's `visibleFountains` and `visibleShops`.
    - Lifetime: the moving pawns last for one animated action and are cleared after its animations; the count overrides last until `beforeNewState()` clears them as the new state publishes, so counts never fall back to the old state between the animation and the publish.

- **History highlight** (`historyHighlight`, derived from the highlighted action, and `highlightedHistoryActionId` in the session):
    - Meaning: where the highlighted History entry took place.
    - Producer: `History` entries through `hoverOrTap`: a mouse or pen shows it while over the entry; a finger tap, Enter or Space toggles it. Consumer: `HistoryHighlight` on the board.
    - Lifetime: a hover highlight ends when the pointer leaves; a toggled one stays until toggled again or another entry takes its place. Either ends when the History tab unmounts, and whenever a new visible state publishes (a History View step, Undo or a live state), so it never outlives the board it described.
    - Validity: drawn on the board being viewed, which in History View is the viewed step.

- **Customer highlight** (`customerHighlight` in the session):
    - Meaning: the player and market colour whose customer count is highlighted.
    - Producer: `PlayerState`'s counter slots through `hoverOrTap`, as for the history highlight. Consumer: `ShopTile`, through `ShopSign`, haloes the matching `PawnCountChip`s.
    - Lifetime: a hover highlight ends when the pointer leaves the slot; a toggled one stays until toggled again or another slot takes its place. Either ends when the Players tab unmounts. It survives History View steps and new states.
    - Validity: it names a player and colour, not shops, so it haloes the matching chips of whatever state is shown, including each History View step.

- **Player aid** (`playerAidOpen` in the session):
    - Meaning: the player aid cards are open over the board.
    - Producer: the header's **?** toggles it; Escape or a click outside the cards closes it. Consumers: `GameTable` lays `PlayerAid` over the board area, and the **?** shows its open state.
    - Lifetime: stays open across state changes and History View steps until closed.

- **Short-queue warning** (`showQueueTooShort` in the session):
    - Meaning: the last queue pawn clicked can't be read as one end and count.
    - Producer: `VisitorQueue`. Consumer: `RefillPanel`.
    - Lifetime: a few seconds, or until a pawn choice is made, a new state publishes, or refilling ends.

## Render ownership

- `Board` owns the layer order: the visitor queue in the margin outside the walls (drawn last instead, above the overlay, while entrances are being refilled), then shops, then fountains, then the shared overlay, which covers only the board and the gate pillars that overhang its outer wall (with their shadows), so the queue margin keeps the page's own background, then the shop signs and fountain visitors (during an auction, whole fountains), which never dim, then whatever is lifted above it (the auctioned or staged shop, the shops the previewed route enters, the selected fountain and its destinations, or the empty entrances awaiting a refill), then the route preview and any history highlight. A route line runs from the edge of its origin fountain to the edge of its destination, never from or to a centre. The count of visitors still waiting sits in the top margin above the board's upper-right corner ("55 waiting"). Pawns moving during a move animation are drawn on a separate transparent layer above the whole board, its own composited layer, so their motion never repaints the filter-heavy board. The overlay blocks clicks on everything beneath it, so only lifted pieces are interactive; the preview and the moving pawns ignore pointer events so they never block a destination.
- The History tab uses the action panel's colours: dark text on its cream background. History entries are one short sentence; a refill shows the pawns it brought, in the order they entered, as one unbreakable group of pawn icons: shops are named by colour in the market colour ("put a yellow shop up for auction", "bought the yellow shop"), never by ID. Payments appear below as rows built from the money report's payments: player tag, a signed amount in green (+) or red (−), then pawns of the customers' colour, an arrow to the player receiving a mover's cut, or "cut" for an auctioneer's cut. An auction entry gives the buyer and price, then the same bid table as the money report: highest bid first, equal bids clockwise from the auctioneer, the order that breaks a tie, so the first listed wins. Hidden text gives screen readers the meaning of each row.
- Local presentation, with no shared state: the bid panel's − and + step 25 Dirham per press and, held down, repeat after 0.5 s and speed up after eight steps, stopping on release, on leaving the button, or at the minimum bid or the player's cash (keyboard activation steps once); action panel buttons darken slightly on hover, a selected refill choice like a filled button.
- Players never see fountain numbers: fountains carry no number labels, and the history describes moves, refills and walk-ins without naming fountains ("You moved 6 visitors."). Screen-reader labels keep the numbers so each fountain stays distinguishable.
- `ShopTile` draws each owned shop's `ShopSign`: a flat cardboard sign in the owner's colour, cut in a shape unique to their seat, with the customer count beside it. That count and the route preview's entering counts use the same `PawnCountChip`: a chip with a pawn and a number on or beside a shop always means visitors of that colour in the shop. Crowded fountains use their own tally panel instead.
- `GameEndPanel` gives the winner line in bold, then one finisher per line as a three-column list: rank, name tag right-aligned, cash left-aligned. Equal cash shares a rank and the next rank skips (1, 1, 3); tied players are listed in seating order.
- `PlayerState` is a card on the action panel's cream with dark text: a strip in the player's colour with name and cash, then the customer counter (a pawn and count per market colour in fixed two-digit slots so it never changes width, colours at zero faded) with "Shops n/6" on the right, then the antiques line ("Antiques n/5" over the viewer's own cards, or "5 hidden antique cards").
- Each antique card shows its colour's item (yellow signet ring, red brass lantern, blue teapot, purple perfume bottle, green curved dagger) above its value. Every card has a border in the vivid market colour. In the owner's hand an item is a white figure on a near-white card until a customer of that colour covers the card; then the item fills with the market colour and the card takes the colour's pale tint. Hands and revealed sets are shown sorted by market colour in the customer counter's order (red, blue, green, purple, yellow), then by value. Clicking anywhere on the owner's hand toggles a line below it listing, in that order with counts, the colours still missing a customer ("Missing: Red, 2x Purple"), or "Set complete" once every card is covered but the set is not yet paid. The owner's revealed set toggles its finishing order instead ("First completed set"). It is local to that player's panel, and only the viewer's own hand is ever drawn, so it reveals nothing about other players' cards.
- `PlayerState` shows a revealed antique set face up to every player, with its award position; cards outside the paid best cards for that position show their items in colour and are dimmed, using the logic's `paidAntiques`.

## Verification scenarios

Scenarios naming a test are checked by the Playwright suite in `tests/`; the rest were checked manually in the dev harness, using scripted browser runs.

1. **Round 2 turn:**
    - At the start, both fountains with visitors and unowned shops are highlighted.
    - Selecting fountain 1 dims the table and leaves only fountain 1 and its destinations 2, 3 and 6 bright; the panel asks for the destination.
    - Hovering fountain 6 previews the route to it.
    - Undo, or clicking fountain 1 again, restores the start-of-turn highlights.
    - Hovering fountain 4 from fountain 8 lifts the yellow and purple shops its visitors would enter, each with a chip counting one visitor.
    - Clicking fountain 6 moves the visitors there.
2. **Entrance refill:**
    - After two moves that empty entrances 1 and 8, the board dims and both empty entrances are highlighted but not yet clickable; the queue stays bright.
    - Choosing the front of the queue and 3 visitors makes both entrances clickable and haloes the three incoming pawns; clicking the fourth pawn from the back instead selects the back and 4, and one Undo clears that choice (`tests/turns.spec.ts`).
    - With 5 or 6 visitors waiting, clicking a middle pawn shows the short-queue warning in the refill panel and changes nothing.
    - Clicking entrance 1 places the visitors and clears the selection.
    - The second refill starts from a fresh selection.
3. **Auction** (`tests/turns.spec.ts`): clicking an unowned shop starts its auction at once and spotlights it until the auction resolves. Undo, before anyone else bids, cancels it; a bidder's Undo takes back their sealed bid.
4. **Outcome line** (`tests/turns.spec.ts`): after the first auction the panel reads "Developer won the yellow shop. Auction an unowned shop." on one line, with no payments or bids; History lists the bids. The next player action replaces it.
5. **Visitor move animation:**
    - From fountain 3 holding blue, red, purple, green and two yellow pawns, with blue, purple, red and yellow shops owned along its routes, moving west to fountain 4 clears the highlights, then walks the pawns single file; purple, red and both yellows turn into their shops, whose chips count up, and blue and green join fountain 4 as fountain 3 empties one pawn at a time.
    - The south and north moves from the same setup branch only the matching shops along those routes.
6. **Undo a move:** Undo straight after that move glides every pawn back to fountain 3 in 200ms, out of the shops and fountain 4, and restores the earlier counts.
7. **Touch move** (phone, `tests/touch.spec.ts`): tapping a destination moves the visitors at once, as a click does; no **Move here** step appears.
8. **Tap toggles:** tapping a customer count haloes that player's matching badges and a second tap clears them; tapping a history entry draws its route and a second tap clears it. Mouse hover still shows each only while over it.
9. **Short-queue warning after a choice:** with six visitors waiting, choosing the front two and then clicking a middle pawn shows the warning in place of the entrance prompt, keeps the front-two choice, and the prompt returns a few seconds later.
10. **Final round:** once the queue empties, the banner names the last seat in turn order ("The game ends after Bashir's turn."), and says "This is the last turn of the game." during that seat's turn.
11. **End standings:** a finished game lists one finisher per line; with cash 5500, 5300, 3700, 3700 the ranks read 1, 2, 3, 3, ties in seating order.
12. **Hold to bid:** a tap on + adds 25; holding + from 100 reaches about 350 after two seconds; holding − stops at the minimum bid.
13. **Turns end by themselves** (`tests/turns.spec.ts`, Admin in the harness): a second move that leaves no entrance to refill passes the turn on at once, with no Confirm turn. Undo then returns the mover to the state after their first move.
14. **History navigation** (`tests/history.spec.ts`):
    - With a fountain selected after a move into owned shops, stepping back hides the staged choice and every intent, glides the pawns home within the 200ms budget, and shows the open auction's spotlight with "All bids are in.".
    - Stepping forward glides the pawns back out; go to current shows the turn with nothing staged.
    - Play history from that step replays the move as a full walk.
15. **Interrupted motion** (`tests/history.spec.ts`): pressing step backwards during a live walk, or step backwards and go to current during a replayed walk, lets the walk finish; the view settles on the moved state with no pawns left in motion and no errors.
16. **Reduced motion** (`tests/history.spec.ts`): with reduced motion requested, a move glides its pawns within the 200ms budget instead of walking, and the active player's border stops pulsing.
17. **Explore under Concealed Cash** (`tests/protected.spec.ts`): the Explore button is disabled in player and spectator views and enabled in Host View, where exploring starts without errors.
18. **Header** (`tests/turns.spec.ts`): the action panel's header reads "Your turn" or "[Player]'s turn" on the left and offers UNDO on the right whenever there is something to undo; no Back button appears anywhere.
19. **Undo after the turn passes on** (`tests/turns.spec.ts`, Admin in the harness): after the placement that empties the last entrance passes the turn on, Undo returns the previous player to the refill with the visitors back in the queue. In a hosted game that player's own Undo does the same until the next player acts; the harness's local seat is always the active player, so these tests reach it through Admin.
20. **Sealed bid corrections** (`src/actions/auction.spec.ts` in the logic package, `tests/turns.spec.ts`): with four players, A starts an auction and bids 300, B bids 200, C bids 150, A undoes, D passes, C undoes, A bids 100 and C bids 250; the auction stays open until that last bid, then C wins at 250. Each Undo takes back only that player's bid and keeps the others'.
21. **Player aid** (`tests/playerAid.spec.ts`): the **?** opens the Your Turn, Money and Antiques cards over the board; Escape or a click outside closes them; with Antique Cards off only Your Turn and Money appear. During an auction the fountains and shop signs are drawn above the dimming overlay.
