# 1880 harness scenarios

Written by `games/1880/src/scenarios.build.spec.ts` (run with `SCENARIOS=1`) from seeded bot
games, each with the actions that led to it.

- **Private auction**: the opening auction of P0 to P7 (§V). Pass with every player and P0 falls
  by ¥5.
- **Choosing investors**: each player in the new seat order takes a foreign investor (§VII).
- **First share round**: majors to start: a director's certificate size, a par, its slot and
  building permits (§XII).
- **Interrupting share round**: a share round brought by the last train of a kind in the middle of
  an operating round (§XII); the company resumes buying trains after it.
- **Investor laying track**: a foreign investor's one tile (§VII).
- **Investor merger**: an investor joined to its company's network; its owner chooses where its
  treasury goes and whether the company's station replaces its own (§VII).
- **Rocket exchange**: the first 4-train forces the Rocket of China's exchange (§VI).
- **Phase C trains**: a major buying trains in phase C.
- **Finished game**: a whole bot game played to its end.
