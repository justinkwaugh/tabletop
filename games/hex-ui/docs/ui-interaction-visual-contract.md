# UI Interaction Visual Contract

## 1. Visual Intents

| Intent    | Trigger                                                                         | Emphasized or interactive                                                                                      | Dimmed  | Unaffected   | Input paths                                                                                         |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| place     | The viewer may place                                                            | Every empty cell is a target; a mouse or pen over one shows a faint fill of the viewer's colour (pointer fill) | Nothing | Filled cells | Tap or click a cell; Tab to it (cyan focus outline) and Enter or Space; touch shows no pointer fill |
| swap      | The viewer is Blue on the turn after Red's first stone, with the swap option on | The prompt's Swap button, beside every empty cell still a target                                               | Nothing | The board    | Tap or click Swap                                                                                   |
| last_move | A stone is on the board and no chain has won                                    | A white hexagon outline inside the latest cell                                                                 | Nothing | Other cells  | None                                                                                                |
| won       | A move joins its player's edges                                                 | A tinted line along the winning chain, edge to edge                                                            | Nothing | Other cells  | None                                                                                                |

## 2. Coexistence and Precedence

The marks on the board come from one derivation, `BoardAnimator.marksOf` with `winOf`; `Board.svelte` draws what they return.

| Intent A  | Intent B       | Can coexist?          | Winner / combination rule                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | -------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| place     | swap           | yes, on the swap turn | Both are offered: a cell placed closes the swap; Swap trades colours and the player now Blue places next                                                   |
| place     | last_move      | yes                   | Pointer fill and targets are on empty cells, the last mark on a filled one, so they never share a cell                                                     |
| swap      | last_move      | yes                   | The mark stays on Red's first stone; swapping leaves it, since no stone is placed                                                                          |
| last_move | won            | no                    | `won` wins: once a chain has won, `marksOf` gives no last mark and the line draws instead; a game called or conceded keeps the last mark and draws no line |
| won       | place          | no                    | Invariant: a won game offers no actions, so `placeable` is empty                                                                                           |
| won       | swap           | no                    | Invariant: no swap turn follows a win, the first stone never joins both edges                                                                              |
| place     | History View   | no                    | `placeable` and `canSwap` are empty while history is viewed; marks and the line show as the viewed state has them                                          |
| place     | a move playing | no                    | The pointer fill hides from the click until the move lands (`updatingVisibleState`), and taps do nothing meanwhile                                         |

## 3. Visual Primitives

- **Board** (`Board.svelte`, `board/geometry.ts`): seen straight down, a rhombus of hexagons with a point at the top, 40 units between neighbouring centres, each row half a cell right of the one above (HexWiki's drawing). Each cell is a tile, a hexagon inset 1.8 units from the cell's outline with its corners rounded over 0.17 of its radius, on a dark ground (`--hex-groove`) that shows as grooves between tiles. Empty tiles are pale (`--hex-cell`). Beyond the cells, each edge has a solid band in its player's colour reaching 0.18 of the spacing past the outermost corners, Red's top and bottom, Blue's left and right; grooves as wide as the tiles' run along where the bands meet the cells, between the bands at the corners and round the rim. At the blunt corners the bands meet at the middle of the corner cell's shared side, at the sharp corners at its corner.
- **Filled cell**: the whole tile in `--hex-red` or `--hex-blue`, flat, with no shading. Never carries interaction state.
- **Upright board** (`upright` title preference, off by default; the turning-arrow toggle beside A1): the whole board turned a quarter anticlockwise, so it stands taller than wide, as suits a phone; a player picks it once and sees the same board on every screen. Labels stay upright and re-clear their bands for the turned board. The player cards' small board icon turns with it.
- **Coordinate labels** (`board/geometry.ts`): HexWiki names in capitals, each standing on the line of cells it names, beyond the band: a letter on its column's slant past the top and bottom rows, a number on its row past the left and right edges, clear of the band by 0.14 of the spacing. The A1 toggle (`showCoordinates` title preference) hides them; the board's frame does not change.
- **Last-move mark**: a white hexagon outline, its corners rounded like a tile's, growing from the cell's centre.
- **Winning line** (`board/winningLine.ts`): the shortest run of the winning chain's cells from edge to edge, through their centres, its bends rounded (0.32 of the spacing), running on 0.7 of the spacing past each end cell into the band, along its last step where that leads off the board. A pale tint of the winner's colour (`--hex-red-tint`, `--hex-blue-tint`), 6.5 units wide, on a white edge 9.5 wide.
- **Player cards** (`PlayerTray.svelte`): the player's colour band, and a small rhombus with their two edges drawn in their colour, turned as the board is.
- **Action panel height** (`ActionPanel.svelte`, `components/holdTallest.ts`): the panel keeps the tallest height it has reached at its current width, so a line that wraps for a long name does not move the board when it shrinks back. A new width starts over; a remount (the end panel, a new game) starts over too.

## 4. Render Ownership

| Layer                                                 | Owns                                                                                             | Allowed Inputs                                  | Must Not Own                |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `HexGameSession`                                      | `placeable`, `canPlace`, `canSwap`; a tap commits a placement or the swap                        | Game state, valid action types                  | Styling                     |
| `Board.svelte`                                        | Hit targets on every empty cell; the pointer fill                                                | Session lists, the animator's cells and marks   | Deciding legality           |
| `BoardAnimator` (`animators/boardAnimator.svelte.ts`) | Cells filling and draining; which mark each cell carries and the winning line, and their motion  | Transition kind, `from`/`to` states, the action | Interaction state, legality |
| `ActionPanel.svelte`                                  | One-line prompt on your turn ("Fill a cell or [Swap]" on the swap turn), the last move otherwise | Session state, history entries                  | Board marks                 |

Draw order inside the board, which the hit targets depend on: ground, bands and grooves, tiles, filled cells, pointer fill, marks, winning line, then the transparent hit targets over everything, so a tap anywhere on a cell's hexagon reaches it. Every cell keeps its target, switched on while the cell is in `placeable` and otherwise ignoring the pointer and hidden from assistive technology: making some 200 targets again as History View ends stalled WebKit. Labels sit outside the board's turn so they stay upright.

## 5. Shared Visual State

- **Targets** (`HexGameSession.placeable`, `canSwap`):
    - Meaning: the cells the viewer may fill now, and whether Swap is offered.
    - Producer: the session, from the displayed state and its valid actions. Consumers: `Board.svelte` (hit targets, pointer fill), `ActionPanel.svelte` (prompt and Swap button).
    - Lifetime and validity: derived, so they follow the state shown; empty when the viewer cannot act, including in History View, and ignored while `updatingVisibleState`.
- **Pointer fill** (`Board.svelte` `hovered`):
    - Meaning: the cell a mouse or pen rests on.
    - Producer: the cell's pointer enter and leave, never a touch. Consumer: `Board.svelte` alone; validity read from the session.
    - Lifetime: cleared on leave and on the click that places. Validity: drawn only while the cell is in `placeable` and no move is playing.
- **Board as drawn** (`BoardAnimator.stones`, `marks`, `win`, `growing`):
    - Meaning: the cells, marks and line the board draws, which lag the published state while a move plays.
    - Producer: `BoardAnimator`, from each listener step's `from` and `to`. Consumer: `Board.svelte`.
    - Lifetime: a step shows both states' cells, marks and lines at once so new ones can come and old ones go; as the move lands (`afterAnimations`) they become the arriving state's and `growing` empties. Validity: the landing writes the board only while the state it began on is still shown, so a later switch of view is never covered.
    - History: steps and jumps in History View animate as below; replays play each action in full; page loads, server syncs and view switches show the board at rest.
- **Layout preferences** (`upright`, `showCoordinates`, title preferences):
    - Meaning: the board turned upright; labels shown.
    - Producer: the session's toggles. Consumers: `Board.svelte`, `PlayerTray.svelte`, `GameTable.svelte`.
    - Lifetime: kept per player across games and screens. A toggle lands any playing move at once first (`moveInterrupter.interrupt`), since its motion was laid out in the old board.
- **Stopping a move** (`HexGameSession.moveInterrupter`, set by `BoardAnimator`): `beforeNewState` abandons the move on `view-switch`; the animator recognises an abandoned presentation's later steps by `presentingState` and skips them.

### Motion

- **Filling** (`board/geometry.ts` `growthOf`, `coverageOf`): a placed cell fills like a liquid in 0.3 s (`power1.out`). From every cell of its colour beside it (the middle of the shared side) and every edge of its player it lies on (its outermost corner), a disc grows; the discs run together where they meet through a cell-sized goo filter (blur 3, alpha cut back to a hard edge) and are clipped to the tile. They grow only until together they cover the cell (plus 0.04), so every fill takes the whole time. A cell touching neither its colour nor its edges spreads from its centre as a soft hexagon (corners rounded over 0.35 of its radius), scaled up, crisp, without the goo. The sources are the cells there before the move.
- **Draining** (Undo, a step back): the discs shrink back into the cells and edge they came from, or the hexagon into its centre, in 0.18 s, the sources the cells still there after.
- **Marks**: a new mark grows from its middle as the fill finishes; the previous move's mark shrinks away as the next cell fills. A draining cell's mark fades over the first half of the drain.
- **Winning line**: drawn along the chain as the winning cell finishes filling, in 0.5 s (`power1.inOut`), Blue's from its left edge to its right and Red's from its bottom edge to its top as the board is first drawn (so on the upright board Blue's bottom to top, Red's right to left); Undo draws it back in 0.18 s. The last-move mark goes as it draws.
- **Steps without an action** (Undo, state-only history, exploration): fills, drains and mark changes all within 0.18 s plus at most 0.01 s waiting for the board to draw.
- **Large jumps**: a step that fills or drains more than 4 cells (`JUMP_LIMIT`) shows them filled or gone at once, with no fill, since WebKit stalls a frame for every few goo-filtered fills starting together; marks and the line still move.
- **Slow frames**: while a move fills a cell, a frame taking longer than 100 ms advances GSAP's clock by only 33 ms (`gsap.ticker.lagSmoothing`, put back to GSAP's 500 ms as the move lands), so a phone that stalls shows the fill late rather than skipping it. Steps without an action keep GSAP's default.
