# South African Railroads

`@tabletop/south-african-railroads` is the deterministic logic package for South African Railroads, a share-auction railroad game by John Bohrer, published by Winsome Games, for 3–6 players. Its companion Game Client package is `@tabletop/south-african-railroads-ui`.

## Rules

The public `Definition` covers the initial offering, the Construct Track, Offer Stock, Develop Settlements and Pay Dividends actions, the opening of the ZASM when track reaches Johannesburg, external connections with their special dividends, and the final payoff after the sixth dividend. Where the rulebook is silent or ambiguous, the choices made are recorded in [rules interpretations](docs/rules-interpretations.md), each backed by a test.

## Maps

Four maps are played, published with John Bohrer's permission: South African Railroads, the original board and the default; Pennsylvania Railroad, with five metro areas and Canadian connections; Veld Railroads, the older South African network with closed borders, a war and a recovery round over eight rounds; and Texas Special, a map by James Geist, who granted permission for this implementation on 17 October 2026. [Maps](docs/maps.md) describes what a map defines and how to add one.
