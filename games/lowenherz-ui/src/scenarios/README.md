# Löwenherz scenarios

The dev harness lists these under **Scenarios**. Each is a four-player game (Ana, Bruno, Carmen and Diego) played by the autoplay helper from `@tabletop/lowenherz/testing` and saved with its whole Action History, so Undo and History work. Loading one again starts it over. Regenerate them with `node scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` after building `@tabletop/lowenherz`.

- **Game over.** A finished 24-round game won by Bruno with 50 power points; Ana holds a Parchment worth 5. Beside the final standings, "Power points by round" draws one line per player, a point at the end of every round and a last point labelled Final. Ana's line steps up 6 into Final: 1 hill point when the King dies and her Parchment worth 5. Point along the chart: the standings become that round's ranking under "After Round N", with the round's leaders in bold, and the chart stays where it is. Point at Final, or move off the chart, and the final table returns with Earned, Parchment and Total.
- **Politics cards from both piles.** Bruno is choosing an action, holding two Alliances taken from the left deck and a Parchment worth 3 taken from the right. The left pile count has a green shadow and the right a blue one; Bruno's Alliances cast green and his Parchment blue, and the other players' face-down cards, all taken from the left deck, cast green and say so on hover. Hover one of Bruno's cards and its magnified copy says along its bottom which deck it came from.
