# 18Chesapeake harness scenarios

Written by `games/18chesapeake/src/scenarios.build.spec.ts` (run with `SCENARIOS=1`) from seeded
bot games, each with the actions that led to it.

- **Private auction**: the opening. Pass with every player before anyone buys and the Delaware
  & Raritan falls by $5; after a purchase, the privates pay and a 2-train is exported (§9).
- **Paired private lay**: a company owning the Columbia – Philadelphia or the Baltimore &
  Susquehanna in its track step. Its two tiles 8 or 9 join across the shared side, besides the
  company's own lay (§7.1).
- **Canal lay**: a company owning the Chesapeake & Ohio Canal in its track step: #57 in Berlin as
  its lay, then a free station there (§7.1).
- **Diesel trade-in**: a company buying trains while D-trains are on sale, $900 or $700 with a
  train traded in (§11.7).
- **Two-player pool purchase**: a two-player stock turn with another president's company in the
  bank pool; buying from it first removes a pool share from the game (§14.3).
- **Off the Rails stock round**: a third stock round of an Off the Rails game, on its own market.
- **Off the Rails finished game**: a whole Off the Rails game.
- **Finished game**: a whole game, for history and the final scores.
