# Promontory Point scenarios

The dev harness lists these under **Scenarios**. Each is a four-seat table (Mara, Royce, Ines and Tobias, in turn order) with Mara's turn open and her pieces face up, set up with no actions behind it; loading one again starts it over. The tables are the named fixtures `TABLE_FIXTURES` in `@tabletop/promontory-point/testing`. Regenerate the files with `node scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` after building `@tabletop/promontory-point`; `src/lib/testing/scenarioFiles.spec.ts` fails when a file and its recipe differ. The numbers are the verification scenarios in `docs/ui-interaction-visual-contract.md`, played by `tests/turnScenarios.spec.ts` (1–15), `tests/repairsScenarios.spec.ts` (16–22) and `tests/tableScenarios.spec.ts` (23, 26–31) at desktop and phone width; 24 and 25 are checked by hand on the tables named below, and 32 and 33 by hand and also by `tests/phone.spec.ts`, `tests/board.spec.ts` and `tests/flights.spec.ts`, as the contract's exercise record says. The Repairs tables open in one browser context per seat, each in the harness's protected mode as that seat; what a seat sends reaches another seat's context by **Save as scenario** and **Load from file…**.

- **Opening turn.** Mara holds CP, KP and KP; every railroad is at $5. Buy a UP share: $15, two UP shares, the deck one fewer and "1 action left"; Undo restores all three (2). Buy twice: every Buy and Sell is dimmed with "no share actions left", and stays so after three placements (5). Place CP: KP is picked by itself, and one Undo takes the CP placement back (8). After the first placement the share block is locked with "Buying and selling open again after your third piece"; after the third it opens with "2 actions left" (9).
- **Buy refused.** Mara has $3 and no SP share, and the other seats hold all eleven SP shares. Southern Pacific's Buy is dimmed with "none left", Central Pacific's with "you have $3"; a press changes nothing (3).
- **Sell at zero.** Union Pacific is on a $0 space. Sell the UP share: money unchanged, one share fewer, the deck one more and "1 action left" (4).
- **Towns in reach.** Central Pacific track runs to a Dividend town, a Repairs town and a +2 town, and Mara holds two CP shares. Press CP: its hexes light, the board dims, chips show on the Dividend and Repairs towns; press it again and nothing is lit. Place CP on the +2 town: CP goes to $7 and the badge "1" appears; Undo returns the piece and the price, and leaves nothing picked and nothing to undo (6). With CP picked, an unlit hex places nothing and an unlit town enlarges its tile (7). Place CP on the Dividend town: Mara gets $4 and every other seat $2, the log reads "Dividend pays you $4, Royce $2, Ines $2, Tobias $2", and Undo takes every payment back (12).
- **Price limits.** Kansas Pacific is at $24 next to a +3 town, Union Pacific on the fourth $0 space next to a −2 town, and Southern Pacific next to Promontory. KP on the +3 town stops at $25 and UP on the −2 town stops on the fifth $0 space, each logged "(capped)" (10). SP on Promontory goes from $5 to $10 and the end tracker reads "Promontory taken ✓" (11).
- **No room.** Southern Pacific track boxes Kansas Pacific in; Mara holds KP, KP and CP, and one CP piece is left in the bag. Both KP pieces read "no room". Pick one: no hex lights and the panel offers only "Set aside · draw a replacement · can't be undone"; send it and the last CP joins her hand face up for everyone, with no Undo, and buying and selling stay closed though nothing is placed yet. Pick the other KP: "Set aside · bag empty"; send it and the turn needs two placements, "Place piece 1 of 2." (13).
- **Pieces placed.** Mara has placed her three pieces. "End turn · draw 3": three pieces fly from the bag to her seat card, the log reads "ended the turn and drew 3" to the other seats, and there is no Undo (14); Royce's turn opens with his hand face up in his seat card and his panel, the others' hands stay hidden, and the header reads "Royce's turn" to the other seats (1).
- **Variant opening turn.** The four-piece variant: Mara holds CP, UP, KP and SP. Place three: "Keep SP · draw 3" keeps SP face up and draws 3; "Return it · draw 4" puts SP back and draws 4 (15).
- **Variant nothing left.** The four-piece variant with three pieces in Mara's hand, so none is left over after three placements: the panel shows "End turn · draw 4" alone, and it draws 4 (15). The state validator allows this hand, though play reaches a three-piece variant hand only as the bag runs dry, when the button would read "bag empty".
- **Repairs all pay.** Southern Pacific track runs to a Repairs town with SP at $5; Mara holds SP, CP and UP. Mara holds two SP shares, Royce one, Ines three, Tobias none, and each has $20. Place SP on the Repairs town: Mara pays $4, Royce $2 and Ines $6 at once, the log reads "bills you $4, Royce $2, Ines $6, all paid", and Mara places on with no stop; Undo takes the placement and every payment back (16).
- **Repairs one short.** As above with SP at $6; Royce holds five SP shares, a CP and a UP and has $7. Place SP on the Repairs town: Royce owes $10 and decides out of turn, "Your turn" in his own view with his three shares at half price; Mara and Ines read "Royce's turn" and the Repairs row with his dashed chip (17). Royce's Sell one on SP reads "Have $7 + sale $3 = $10 of $10 · enough", locks every other Sell and offers "Sell 1 SP share · pay $10"; Undo pops it; the send leaves him $0 and Mara places on (18). While he stages, Mara undoes her placement: the Repairs step is gone and so are his staged sales (21). Nothing he stages reaches another seat; after the send the log reads "sold 1 SP at half price ($3) and paid $10" (22).
- **Repairs two short.** As above with SP at $5; Mara owes $6 with $3 and Ines $4 with $1. Mara, the placing seat, decides first and Ines waits with "waiting" in the row and no panel; two SP sales pay Mara's $6, and then Ines decides (19).
- **Repairs short after everything.** As above with SP at $2; Royce holds four SP shares and has $1, so he owes $8. Nothing is sold for him: one Sell one stages all four, "Sell all 4 · pay $5 · $3 forgiven", and only his own send pays; he ends at $0, still in the game (20). The contract's other case, a short seat with no shares ("Pay $3 · $5 forgiven"), cannot occur in play, since a short seat always holds the railroad that billed it.
- **Money wrap.** Mara has $97 and one UP share at $5. Sell it: her marker steps forward through 99 and 0 to the 2 space with "+100" beside it, and her seat card reads $102; Undo brings it back to 97 (23).
- **Shared spaces.** Mara and Royce have $20, Ines and Tobias $12; CP and UP are at $7, KP and SP at $5. Each pair of seats sits side by side on its money space, and each pair of railroads in its own slots on its price (24, by hand; every other table puts all four seats on the 20 space as a 2×2 grid).
- **Two red tiles left.** Promontory is taken and every red tile but two is covered; Central Pacific track runs to one of the two, a Dividend town. Covered red towns keep their red border around the piece, and the end tracker reads 2 (25, by hand). Place CP on the Dividend town and two more pieces, and End turn ends the game: Mara and Royce tie at $34, both named and drawn in amber (29).
- **Bag running out.** Two pieces are left in the bag. Place three, and "End turn · draw 2" empties the bag and ends the game: Ines wins with $51 (29).
