# 1833NE harness scenarios

Written by `scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` (build `@tabletop/1833ne` first). Four players; the
privates were sold at face in the auction and the B&W and B&P launched at $150.

## First operating round

The B&W operates first, though both corporations stand at $150: the game's first operating
round runs in reverse price order, and a column still runs top to bottom.

- Lay track: only Framingham (F23) is open to the B&W, its first-round reserved hex; the
  B&P's Mansfield (G24) is closed to it.
- Issue shares: the B&W may issue up to two at $137.
- Place a station in Worcester after laying a tile there: it costs $40, not $80.

## Ferries in the second round

The rulebook's OR1 has been played (p. 8): the B&W reaches Worcester with a 2 and a 2+1, the
B&P owns the Stonington ferry and a 2 and a 2+1.

- B&W: issue a share (it has $45), lay E24 and Norwich (E26), buy the Allyn's Point ferry; its 2+1 earns $60 to Boston and
  its 2 earns $80 to Norwich with the ferry and New bonus.
- B&P: buy the Providence ferry; its 2+1 earns $90 to Boston's terminal and its 2 earns $80 to
  Kingston, both carrying the New bonus on Providence.
