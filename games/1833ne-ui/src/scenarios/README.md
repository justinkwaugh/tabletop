# 1833NE harness scenarios

Written by `scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` (build `@tabletop/1833ne` first). Four players; the
privates were sold at face in the auction and the B&W and B&P launched at $150.

## First operating round

The B&W operates first, though both corporations stand at $150: the game's first operating
round runs in reverse price order, and a column still runs top to bottom.

- Lay track: only Framingham (F23) is open to the B&W, its first-round reserved hex; the
  B&P's Mansfield (G24) is closed to it.
- Issue shares: the B&W may issue up to two at $137.
- Place a station in Worcester after laying a tile there: it costs $40, not $80.

## Ferries in the second round

The rulebook's OR1 has been played (p. 8): the B&W reaches Worcester with a 2 and a 2+1, the
B&P owns the Stonington ferry and a 2 and a 2+1.

- B&W: issue a share (it has $45), lay E24 and Norwich (E26), buy the Allyn's Point ferry; its 2+1 earns $60 to Boston and
  its 2 earns $80 to Norwich with the ferry and New bonus.
- B&P: buy the Providence ferry; its 2+1 earns $90 to Boston's terminal and its 2 earns $80 to
  Kingston, both carrying the New bonus on Providence.

## Takeover step

A Takeover game, in outline the rulebook's takeover example (p. 15): Player 2 launched the B&W
at $80 and Player 3 the Hartford at $40; the Hartford built to Worcester's east (D23) and the
B&W west of Boston to Worcester. The phase I trains sold out and the B&W bought the first 3/5,
so the takeover step follows OR2. The B&W has $100, the Hartford $105, a 2 train and its
president's two shares outstanding at $20.

- Player 2: choose the Hartford. The panel offers its two shares for $40 cash; step a share
  across to a B&W swap and the cash falls by $20. Take over the Hartford.
- Player 3 is asked: the Hartford runs a 2, so it may refuse. Accept: the Hartford closes, a
  B&W token replaces its Hartford token, the B&W's price drops from $60 to $50 (the Hartford's
  was lower), its train limit becomes 4, a phase IV train leaves the depot and the certificate
  limit drops to 25.
- Refuse instead: the Hartford's turn follows, and it may take over the B&W by swapping its
  own shares for Player 2's.

## Variable Start railroads

The takeover step above with both passing: the second stock round, in phase II.

- Start: the five Variable Start railroads (B&M, NYNH&H, New Era, Uncertain Prospects, LSL)
  are offered. Choose one and the open cities light up on the map; Worcester (the B&W's
  reserved spot) and the homes of corporations yet to start do not.
- Choose Springfield (D21): its hex has no track, so the home costs $80 and any starting price
  from $40 is offered. Choose Manchester (F17) instead: it has track, so the home costs $120
  and only prices from $60 are offered.
