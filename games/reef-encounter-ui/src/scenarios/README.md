# Reef Encounter scenarios

The dev harness lists these under **Scenarios**. Each opens on the first player's turn of a two-player game on boards 3 and 4, just after setup; loading one again starts it over. Regenerate them with `node scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` after building `@tabletop/reef-encounter`.

- **Consumed polyps to spend.** The first player holds a consumed white and a consumed pink polyp. The panel offers 6 Exchange and 7 Acquire for each. Acquire with the white: choosing a colour rings the open sea's cylinder circle and every tile the cylinder may lock, and hides the larva-space rings; Undo steps back to the colour, then to the turn's choices. Put it on the cylinder circle: the matching coral tiles turn over.
- **Coral ready to eat.** The first player's shrimp sits on a white coral of five on Board 1. The panel offers 1 Eat; choosing it rings the coral in red and hides every other ring, and Undo closes the choice. Eat it: four whites go back into the bag, one goes into their parrotfish, and their shrimp takes the first silhouette on the open sea.
- **Last orange larva cube.** The general supply has no orange larva cube left. Take the orange space: the open sea cannot be refilled, so the game ends at once and the score table shows.
- **Bag searches.** The pink and white bonus spaces are empty. A warning below the tiles says trading for either searches the bag, and the pink trade carries a ! mark. Exchange (8) the pink larva cube: the history reads that the bag held a pink polyp, and Undo is gone, since searching the bag is a reveal. Then play the white cube and place a white polyp on Board 1, row 3, column 3, beside the starfish square (marked !): the bag is searched again and a white polyp grows on the starfish square. Alternatively, end the turn at once: the refill fills both bonus spaces and the warning goes.
- **Both polyp sources.** The first player holds a white larva cube, five white polyps in their supply and a consumed white polyp. The Play tile offers five polyps, since one cube places at most four from the supply. Play the white cube and choose a square: a popup beside it asks whether the polyp comes from the supply or is the consumed one, and Undo closes it without placing anything. Choose Consumed: the polyp is placed and the consumed one is spent; the next square places from the supply with no popup.
