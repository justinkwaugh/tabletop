# Stellar Horizons II: animation notes

Ideas for animations to add later. Nothing here is built. When one is taken up, follow `.agents/skills/game-ui-animation/SKILL.md`.

## Transfer panel

1. **Scrapping.** When cargo is moved to Scrap, the item leaves the ship tile and travels to the scrap area.
    - Fun option: draw the scrap area as a black hole, with the item stretched and pulled in as it arrives.

## Star map

2. **Ship destroyed.** An explosion on the ship's pip when it is lost: an RE to a malfunction, or a CV that takes damage equal to its size (from a malfunction now, and from combat in the campaign).
3. **Combat hits.** Once a combat is settled, a torpedo flies from the attacking ship to the ship it hit, one for each hit.
4. **Movement.** A ship travels from its system to its destination when it moves.
