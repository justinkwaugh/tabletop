# Ride the Rails open questions

Every place the digest is silent, ambiguous or self-contradictory, and every place two readings would make different code. Each has a recommended ruling. Once answered, each becomes an `R-x.y-H<n>` entry in `rulings.md` and is struck from here.

**Scope of the first release:** the USA map only. The expansion maps (R-8.2 to R-8.5) come later, and with them Q9, Q10, Q11, Q12, Q13, Q17, Q18, Q21, Q23 and the expansion halves of Q3 and Q16; those stay here, deferred, until then. Q1 is settled for now: one map, so no map option yet.

Questions marked _(map)_ need the map boards before they can be settled; the rest can be settled from the rules alone.

## How to read a question

- **Q<n> <short title>** (R-x.y): the question.
    - _Readings:_ the two or more ways the rule could go, and what each changes in play.
    - _Sources:_ what the rulebook, FAQ and errata each say, quoted.
    - _Recommendation:_ the reading to take and why, or "provisional" when only the publisher can settle it.

## Setup

- **Q1 Map choice** (R-8): how is the map picked?
    - _Readings:_ (a) a table option at game creation, one map per game, default USA; (b) random.
    - _Sources:_ each expansion is a separate board "to expand your game"; nothing on choosing.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), a creation option with the five maps, default USA.

- **Q2 Player counts** (R-1.2): 3 to 5 only?
    - _Readings:_ (a) 3–5 as printed; (b) add an unofficial 2-player or 6-player mode.
    - _Sources:_ every rulebook says 3–5; placement limits exist only for 3, 4 and 5.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). There are no limits to give other counts.

- **Q3 Passenger count** (R-1.3) _(map)_: does every city get a passenger, and is the supply limited?
    - _Readings:_ (a) one per city, supply unlimited (Paris Travels never runs out); (b) supply capped at 52.
    - _Sources:_ B "one Passenger on each city"; the box has 52; FD adds a passenger to Paris each round.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). Check each map's city count against 52 once transcribed; if a map has more than 52 cities, ask again.

## Turn structure

- **Q4 Turn-order ties** (R-2.5): what does "swap player positions" do for players tied on money?
    - _Readings:_ (a) tied players reverse their relative order from the round just played; (b) tied players keep their relative order.
    - _Sources:_ B p2 "In the case of ties, swap player positions."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). "Swap" names a change; for three or more tied, reverse their previous order.

- **Q5 No legal journey** (R-3.3.1): what if a player has no passenger they can move?
    - _Readings:_ (a) the player passes phase 3 and earns nothing; (b) impossible by design, so no rule needed.
    - _Sources:_ B "each player must transport a Passenger"; silent on the case. Late in a round on a sparse network, the passengers left may all sit on cities no link reaches.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The engine offers a pass only when no legal journey exists.

- **Q6 Locomotive supply** (R-1.1, R-3.1, R-3.2): is the supply of 27 per railroad a hard limit on shares and track together?
    - _Readings:_ (a) hard limit: once all 27 of a colour are on boards and the map, no more shares or track of it; (b) unlimited.
    - _Sources:_ B "162 Locomotives (27 each of 6 colors)"; shares and track draw on the same supply; no rule on running out.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), provisional. It is the physical game, and it is rarely reached (Red and Blue at 5 players are the likeliest). Phase 1's "must take" then means "must take one of an available railroad that still has supply".

## Actions

- **Q7 Share limits** (R-3.1): any cap on shares of one railroad per player?
    - _Readings:_ (a) none (six rounds caps a player at six shares in all); (b) the player board's row length caps it.
    - _Sources:_ B "one locomotive of any available color"; the player board picture shows rows of spaces but the text sets no limit.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a).

- **Q8 $5 cities and starts** (R-4.3.2): after a railroad starts in a $5 city, can a later railroad building into it still earn the $5?
    - _Readings:_ (a) yes: a start isn't "building into" the city, so the first railroad to build into it from a neighbouring hex earns $5; (b) no: the first locomotive in the city takes the bonus slot, and a start forfeits it.
    - _Sources:_ B "gives $5 to the first player to build track into that city. No bonus is given if another Railroad builds into the city, or if a Railroad starts in the city."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), provisional. It reads "into" literally and matches Canada's distinction between building into Toronto ($3) and starting there ($2).

- **Q9 Starting in Paris or Berlin** (R-8.4.3, R-8.5.3): does a railroad's first placement in Paris or Berlin earn the city bonus?
    - _Readings:_ (a) yes, as Chicago's $2 includes a start; (b) no, as the $5 cities exclude starts.
    - _Sources:_ B (Chicago) "This is also true when building track for the first time with a new Railroad"; FD "Whenever a player builds track into Paris/Berlin" with no word on starts; AC prints a separate, smaller start amount for Toronto.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), provisional. These are unlimited cities like Chicago, and Chicago's rule is the base game's default for them.

- **Q10 Germany: Orange in a mountain** (R-8.5.2, R-3.2.4): when a player builds Orange in a mountain and another railroad elsewhere the same turn, is the turn's limit reduced?
    - _Readings:_ (a) only a non-Orange placement on a mountain reduces the limit; (b) any mountain placement reduces it unless the player builds only Orange.
    - _Sources:_ FD "The Orange Railroad may build track in a Mountain hex without the -1 placement penalty."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The exemption attaches to the Orange placement.

- **Q11 Australia: long-distance hexes** (R-8.2.4, R-8.2.5): "consumes the player's entire track placement turn": must it be the turn's only placement?
    - _Readings:_ (a) only placement: allowed only as the first placement, and it ends the turn; (b) allowed as the last placement after others.
    - _Sources:_ AC "Building track in one of these hexes consumes the player's entire track placement turn."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). "Entire" means the whole turn's allowance.

- **Q12 Australia: Perth and Brisbane "points"** (R-8.2.5): are the 50 and 20 dollars?
    - _Readings:_ (a) dollars, added to money; (b) a separate score.
    - _Sources:_ AC "receive 50 points" / "20 points"; the game has no score other than money (B, Ending the game).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). Both cities hold one locomotive, so each pays once per game.

- **Q13 Australia and Canada mountains and limits** (R-8.2.3, R-8.3.1): does Australia keep the base limits 8 / 5 / 4 when no mountain is used?
    - _Readings:_ (a) yes, base limits, reduced to 6 / 3 / 2 with a mountain; (b) other limits printed on the board.
    - _Sources:_ AC lists only the reduced Australian limits; Canada states its own (7 / 5 / 4).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). Australia uses the base rules except where listed, and only the reduction is listed. Check the board's reminder strip when transcribed.

## Starting railroads

- **Q14 Yellow "and/or"** (R-4.2.3): may Yellow start twice, once in Chicago and once in a westernmost city?
    - _Readings:_ (a) yes: two starting placements, so Yellow may have two separate networks; (b) one start only, in either place.
    - _Sources:_ B "must start in Chicago and/or in one of the three westernmost occupied cities."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), provisional. "And/or" names both. The second start may be made in any later turn; the westernmost set stays the one fixed at Yellow's first placement.

- **Q15 Westernmost cities** (R-4.2.3): how are "the three westernmost occupied cities" found?
    - _Readings:_ rank occupied cities (holding at least one locomotive) by how far west their hex lies: (a) the three furthest west, with every city tied with the third included; (b) ties broken by some other order (north first).
    - _Sources:_ B "one of the three westernmost occupied cities. Prior to building, the westernmost city must have one, and only one, locomotive in the hex."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). West is the hex's horizontal position on the board. A city among the three that already holds two locomotives (or is a one-locomotive city already filled) can't take Yellow; the three are still counted with it.

- **Q16 Black's start** (R-4.2.5): "any city that does not already contain 2 locomotives": does that let Black start in a one-locomotive city (USA East Coast, France's Red/Blue/Black cities) that is still empty, and in a city that shows no Black?
    - _Readings:_ (a) any city with room for Black under its capacity, whatever colours it shows, plus Chicago; (b) only cities that show Black.
    - _Sources:_ B "may start in any city that does not already contain 2 locomotives, or it may also start in Chicago."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The wording is "any city"; capacity still applies, and an East Coast start still counts toward Black's two East Coast cities.

- **Q17 "Chicago" on the other maps** (R-4.2) _(map)_: Orange, Yellow and Black name Chicago as a start. On maps without Chicago, does the map's unlimited city (Toronto, Paris, Berlin) take its place?
    - _Readings:_ (a) no substitute: start colours printed on the board decide; (b) the unlimited city stands in for Chicago.
    - _Sources:_ AC and FD don't say; Germany names Berlin for Purple only; AC says Australia and Canada introduce railroads in new ways.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), to confirm against the boards' printed start colours.

- **Q18 Railroad introduction on Australia and Canada** (R-1.4) _(map)_: which railroads arrive when, and where they start.
    - _Readings:_ whatever the boards print.
    - _Sources:_ publisher blurb only; AC rules silent.
    - _Recommendation:_ transcribe from the boards; then ask again only where a board's symbols are unclear.

## Bonuses

- **Q19 Transcontinental: what counts** (R-4.3.3): does one railroad crossing coast to coast count, and what is a "connection"?
    - _Readings:_ (a) at least two railroads; two railroads connect where both have a locomotive in the same hex; adjacent locomotives of different railroads don't connect; (b) one railroad alone also counts.
    - _Sources:_ B "a line of track comprised of different Railroads connects a West Coast Purple & Black city to one of the six multi-colored East Coast cities… If the Railroads connect in a city hex, the bonus is $12… in any other hex, the bonus is $8."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The text says "different Railroads" and prices the place where they connect.

- **Q20 Transcontinental: $12 or $8** (R-4.3.3): with three or more railroads in the line and junctions of both kinds, which price applies?
    - _Readings:_ (a) $12 if some completed line changes railroad only in city hexes, else $8; (b) priced by the junction the completing placement made.
    - _Sources:_ as Q19.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The player is paid for the best line they completed. The bonus goes to the player whose placement first completes any qualifying line.

- **Q21 Intercolonial and Greater Germany** (R-8.3.4, R-8.5.5): what does "connect all the cities… only complete links" require?
    - _Readings:_ (a) every named city can reach every other through complete links of any railroads, changing railroad only in cities (as a passenger could travel); (b) one railroad must reach them all.
    - _Sources:_ AC "Only complete links may be used to collect the bonus"; FD the same.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The bonus goes to the player whose placement first completes it.

## Ride the Rails

- **Q22 Links and cities on the way** (R-3.3.2, R-3.3.5): when one railroad's track runs through a city, is that two links, and may a route change railroad only in a city?
    - _Readings:_ (a) a link runs between two cities with no city between them, so every city passed through is visited and counted; railroads change only in cities; (b) a single railroad's run from start to end is one link however many cities it passes.
    - _Sources:_ B "A Link is a path of locomotives belonging to a single Railroad that connects two cities"; "add the total number of Links… and add 1. This is the number of cities visited, including the starting and ending cities."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). Only (a) makes "links + 1 = cities visited" true.

- **Q23 Impassable hexsides and estuaries** (R-8.3.5, R-8.4.5): how do they act?
    - _Readings:_ (a) the two hexes are not adjacent for building, for a link's path, or for the bonus connections; (b) something narrower.
    - _Sources:_ AC/FD "may not directly build track across… may not carry Passengers directly across."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a).

## Information and undo

Everything is public (R-5). The one random step is the initial turn order, made at setup.

- **Q24 Undo** (R-5): what may a player take back?
    - _Readings:_ (a) any of their own actions until the next player acts (each placement, with any bonus it paid; a share; a journey); (b) nothing.
    - _Sources:_ none; nothing in the game reveals information.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). With nothing hidden or random after setup, the platform's usual undo applies.

## Game end and scoring

- **Q25 Ties** (R-6.2): how is a tie for most money settled?
    - _Readings:_ (a) tied players share the win; (b) a tie-break (turn order, shares held).
    - _Sources:_ B "Ties are possible."
    - _Recommendation:_ (a).
