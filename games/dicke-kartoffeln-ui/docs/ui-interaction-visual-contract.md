# Dicke Kartoffeln UI interaction visual contract

## Visual intents

- **Plan a round.** While a farmer is deciding, the fields they may act on carry a dashed yellow outline with a glow. Clicking one adds to the draft: a worm taken (compost round), a furrow (ploughing), fertilizer or a poison cloud. Draft pieces are drawn faded with a dashed edge; a compost pile shows the change it will receive.
- **The yield sheet.** Sowing, fertilizing and offering open the yield sheet. Only the cells that take input are tinted yellow; no row is tinted. A draft is written in pencil (a dashed grey circle around A, B or C, a bright blue F, grey step counts), and everything the record holds is in blue ink. A sale box with sacks offered in the draft is tinted blue with a blue edge. The sale boxes keep a fixed height, so the black SALE bar never moves. "Offer all my sacks" offers every harvested sack as one edit, organic crops to whichever of Bio or Norm pays more now. Once a year's sowing is revealed, that year's profit columns keep prices only for the varieties the farmer sowed. Beside the form, a folded copy of the potato market (one shared demand and price scale, each variety's demand and price markers, Norm dark and Bio light) keeps the market in view while entering. On narrow screens the sheet is a narrow form drawn near full size: a strip of years with each year's profit in ink (the current year tinted, the shown year black), then one year's page with a row per field (seed letters, yield, and the Norm and Bio sale boxes under black heads) with targets large enough to tap. It shows the current year; tapping a year in the strip shows that year until the year changes. The profit calculation columns are left out, and the potato market, yields and reckoning boxes stack below. The sheet is written from Action metadata only.
- **Sow from the farm.** Clicking a ploughed field instead opens a seed picker beside it with the three seed bags and their prices. Bubbles on each bag give the field's yield from the chart at its worms: a dark one for the sacks with fertilizer and, when the field kept clean will have three sunflowers by the harvest, a green one for the Bio sacks unfertilized; a legend names them. Messages are not counted. The picker is drawn outside the board's zoom, so it keeps its size at any scale. On narrow screens it opens below or above the field, whichever has more board, with finger-sized bags, and stays wholly on the board below the action area, covering part of the field if it must. It belongs to the own large farm: showing the yield sheet or another farm hides it.
- **Seal.** The action panel prices the draft, warns when it needs a loan, and offers "Seal my plan", or "Do nothing this round" for an empty draft.
- **Waiting.** A farmer who has sealed sees their plan and who is still deciding; player signs show "deciding…" or "plan sealed" from public submission records.
- **Messages.** The three face-up message cards and this year's card sit under the market; this year's card is framed in red. Clicking one opens the same card enlarged (one layout at a design size, scaled, so the lines never change), with the die roll that triggers it above and arrows to page through the visible cards.
- **The year track.** The action area shows the round in play and as many following rounds as fit its width comfortably (up to a whole year), running on into next year (set off by a divider) while one follows. Narrow screens drop the round names and show only the next few.
- **Player aid.** The "?" beside Undo opens the English edition's Annual Review sheet as text over the board, two equal pages side by side. On narrow screens it covers the turn header and action area as well as the board, and reads as one scrolling page: smaller type, no indents, the yields and price tables at the text's width, and the close button staying in view. In full screen it opens inside the full-screen view.
- **The close of the year.** Rounds 9 to 12 resolve on their own. A farmer's first decision after a year ends waits behind a table of every farmer's results for those rounds; the arrows reveal one round at a time with its rule, the track follows the round shown, and "Start year N" (or "skip") opens the plan. In History View each of rounds 9, 10, 11 and 12 is its own history step, and the action area shows the table up to the round reached.
- **Compost piles.** Each pile on the large farm shows the year whose round 10 its worms can be spread in, or "scores at end" past the last year.
- **Other farms.** While another farmer's farm is large, its caption offers "show my farm".

## Coexistence and precedence

Only one planning round is live at a time, owned by its machine state. Only the viewer's own large farm and their own sheet row take input; the small farms only switch the large farm. The message dialog sits above the board and closes on any click.

## Shared visual state

The session owns the plan draft (an edit list), the seed-picker field, the shown farm, the shown sheet, the open message, and the year-close page and whether it has been gone through.

- **Draft model:** the draft is an unordered edit log in which a field can be clicked any number of times, which the stage-ordered staged-selection helpers cannot represent, so it is kept as its own list; Undo still pops one manual edit at a time before undoing actions.
- **Lifetime:** the draft and seed picker clear in `beforeNewState` except on `other-action` and `reconciliation`, so another farmer sealing a plan leaves the draft standing. Undo removes the latest edit, then undoes actions.
- **Validity:** the draft is keyed by farmer, year and round and reads as empty outside the round that made it, so a leftover draft can never be submitted elsewhere.
- **Year close:** the page and the "gone through" mark are keyed by the round-9 action of the year shown, so they lapse on their own when the next year closes. While the close is pending the farmer is not yet planning: the board takes no clicks and no draft exists. It is a view, not a selection, so Undo does not step through it.

## Render ownership

The farm art is beneath the furrow card (a cardboard tile whose ink-and-watercolour print is drawn from the field number, so it looks the same on every render), beneath the counters, beneath the harvest label and the click target. No animation runs on the board.
