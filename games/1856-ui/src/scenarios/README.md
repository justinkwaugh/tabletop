# 1856 harness scenarios

Written by `games/1856/src/scenarios.build.spec.ts` (run with `SCENARIOS=1`) from seeded bot
games, each with the actions that led to it.

- **Private auction**: the opening. Buy the next private at its cost or bid $5 more on a later
  one; when everyone passes the privates pay and the Flos Tramway costs $5 less.
- **Loan step**: a company with loans at the end of its turn, where it may redeem them (Redeem
  Government Loans).
- **CGR formation**: the first 6-train's buyer has ended its turn; a president decides whether to
  repay their company's remaining loans or let the CGR absorb it.
- **CGR stock round**: a stock round after the CGR has formed.
- **Finished game**: a whole game, for history and the final scores.
