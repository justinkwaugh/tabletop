## System train help

Blair presides over AMTK, a System formed from the ACL and the CG, at its train step in phase 4, where one shell holds three trains. AMTK owns one 4-train and has $100; Blair has $600. The next train is a $300 4-train, which AMTK cannot afford alone (§11.6.7).

- The train list keeps **finish**, and the 4-train is marked "Blair adds $200". Choosing it buys the train with all of AMTK's $100 and $200 of Blair's.
- AMTK then has two trains and no cash, and Blair $400, so the 4-train is offered again with "Blair adds $300".
- After that second help Blair has $100, too little to help again, so AMTK's turn ends.
- History shows each as "Helped buy a train".
