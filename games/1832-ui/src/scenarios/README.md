# 1832 scenarios

The dev harness lists these under **Scenarios**; loading one again starts it over. `scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` writes them; build the logic package first.

## System train help

Blair presides over AMTK, a System formed from the ACL and the CG, at its train step in phase 4, where one shell holds three trains. AMTK owns one 4-train and has $100; Blair has $600. The next train is a $300 4-train, which AMTK cannot afford alone (§11.6.7).

- The train list keeps **finish**, and the 4-train is marked "Blair adds $200". Choosing it buys the train with all of AMTK's $100 and $200 of Blair's.
- AMTK then has two trains and no cash, and Blair $400, so the 4-train is offered again with "Blair adds $300".
- After that second help Blair has $100, too little to help again, so AMTK's turn ends.
- History shows each as "Helped buy a train".

## Price protection

Alex has sold two CG shares in stock round 2 and ended their turn, so Blair, CG's president, decides whether to buy them back at $100 or let the price drop to $82 (§5.9).

- As Blair, the action panel asks to Protect or Decline.
- In Non-active view (Alex), the panel reads "Waiting for blair" with a Price protection line naming the sale and both prices, above Alex's sale as the last action.

## Excess trains

Blair's CG has bought the first 5-train, starting phase 5 and leaving CG and the ACL over the limit of 2. Blair has discarded for CG; Alex, the ACL's president, must now discard during Blair's turn.

- As Alex, the action panel offers the ACL's trains to discard.
- In Non-active view (Blair), the panel reads "Waiting for alex" with "Atlantic Coast Line must discard 1 train to its limit of 2", above "Central of Georgia discarded a train" as the last action.

## Train offer
