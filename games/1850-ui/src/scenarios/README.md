# 1850 scenarios

The dev harness lists these under **Scenarios**; loading one again starts it over. `scripts/build-scenarios.mjs` writes them from the recorded game in `games/1850/test/fixtures`; build the logic package first.

## Bridge held by a player

A company's first operating rounds, with a player holding the Mississippi River Bridge Company. St. Louis takes no tile while they do (§15.1).

## Price protection

A player has sold shares and ended their turn, so the company's president may buy them back or let the price fall (§5.9).

## Company shares

A president's stock turn with a company that may redeem (§5.10). **Company action** in the stock panel lists the shares it may redeem.

## Mesabi token

A company whose track reaches the Mesabi Range, once a company owns the Mesabi Mining Company or it has closed. The action panel offers a token for $80, using the yellow tile lay (§6.5.1).

## Edge token

A company in its station step whose track reaches its red edge area. The action panel offers the $50 token, as the turn's token placement (§7.4).

## Extra lay

A company with a private's extra yellow lay open, after its ordinary lay (§6.1.2, §15.2).

## Late game

A company's track step in phase 10: grey tiles, last off-board values, edge tokens on the map.
