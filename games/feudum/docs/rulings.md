# Feudum rulings

The code cites the rules as `R-<section>` of `rules-digest.md`. A citation ending in `-H<n>` names one of the house rulings below, made where the rules are silent or ambiguous. `R-X.<n>` names a rule of this implementation. A ruling marked _provisional_ awaits the publisher's answer; it stands until answered. Each ruling names the spec that covers it.

Rules version: the December 2018 English rulebook with the designer's later answers. Each ruling names the question it settled, `design` for one settled while designing the engine and UI, or `rulebook` or `FAQ` for one that follows a passage of either the engine once missed; a question's readings and sources were set out in `open-questions.md` when it was asked. `revised` marks a ruling changed since it was first made, to follow the rulebook.

## House rulings

### Components and setup (R-1)

- **R-1.1-H1** (Q3): shillings are limited to the 78 in the box (50 coins), like every other component; a payment from the supply when it runs short pays what remains. Rejected: unlimited money. Spec: `supply.spec.ts`.
- **R-1.2-H1** (Q25, Q27): the routes are read from the printed board. Roads are its drawn paths and ferries its facing icons; ship, submersible and flying-machine routes are its trails of the textures the rulebook's legend names for those vessels. The rulebook moves a vessel "along the vessel route matching the vessel" and its legend names ship, submersible and flying-machine routes, and the designer rules a northern mountain location out of ship reach, so free movement between adjacent spaces is rejected. Spec: `boardMap.spec.ts`.
- **R-1.4-H1** (Q2): 2 to 5 players; with 2 or 3 players, dummy Knight rows are added in unused colours until there are exactly 4 rows. Rejected: a dummy row for every unused colour. Spec: `initializer.spec.ts`.
- **R-1.4-H2** (Q26): at setup each epoch's six region tiles are shuffled, one into each region's stack, and a tile's landscape stays hidden until it is taken. Rejected: fixed stacks with a set landscape per tile. Spec: `regionTiles.spec.ts`.
- **R-1.4-H3** (Q28), _provisional_: the rosary unlimited and 4 chicken spaces, until the printed board or a designer ruling settles them. Spec: `provisional.spec.ts`.

### Actions (R-3)

- **R-3.1-H1** (Q4): all players choose their hands at the same time, and each finalises ("Long live the king") whenever they like, in any order. Until every player has finalised, no player learns another's pouch good, whether one was committed, or the size of another's hand: the table sees only who has finalised, and a committed good stays in its owner's supply, known to its owner alone. When the last player finalises, `RevealPouches` reveals every pouch good and hand size at once, each committed good leaves its supply for the pouch, and the cards begin. A player yet to finalise may act at will (R-4.4-H1); one who has finalised may not. A round's finalises share one simultaneous group (`handSelectionGroup`), so a finalise sent from a state another player's finalise has since moved on is still taken. Rejected: finalising in turn order with each pouch good public as it is finalised, which follows the rulebook's later player waiting on an earlier one's pouch good but makes every player finalise in seat order (see `known-deviations.md`). Spec: `selectHand.spec.ts`, `turnOrder.spec.ts`, `visibility.spec.ts`, `completeGame.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.2-H1** (Q5): a player who has played all chosen cards is skipped until everyone has. Spec: `turnOrder.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.2-H2** (design): a turn ends by itself once its card, a declared second card and their follow-ups are done, after any seat over the writ limit has discarded down (R-4.4-H9). Rejected: an End Turn press every turn. Spec: `turnOrder.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.3-H1** (design): the sequential action is declared with the first of its two cards. Rejected: offering it after every card while the sulfur is unused. Spec: `turnOrder.spec.ts`, `actions.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.4-H1** (Q1): every game uses the advanced rules; there is no basic-game option. Rejected: a basic-game table option. Spec: `actions.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.6-H1** (design): a pawn migrating off leaves its vessel abandoned on the space (R-4.2.2), and its wine's sulfur goes to the haversack. Spec: `migrate.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.7-H1** (design): only the first placement on the epic voyage takes a free disc; a disc already on the track steps on without one. Rejected: asking for a free disc at every step. Spec: `placeDisc.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.7-H2** (design): a target pilfered from an archery butt becomes one of the seat's markers from the general supply, as a collected target does (R-4.3.4), and goes at once onto any guild track the seat qualifies for but had no marker for (R-4.5.2); with none of the seat's markers left there it returns to the supply and gives nothing. Rejected: refusing the pilfer. Spec: `move.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.7-H3** (design): a Move with no step and no Godspeed is a legal play of the card and is not a successful move. Rejected: requiring a step, since "Successful move" presumes a Move can be unsuccessful. Spec: `move.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.8-H1** (design): Influence places all its markers, then status recomputes once. Short of markers, the player places exactly as many as the supply holds, on spaces they choose. Rejected: recomputing between markers; holding a marker back. Spec: `influence.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.8-H2** (design): Money where the seat already has its own serf costs 1s for what plain Influence would give; it is accepted, but not offered. Spec: `influence.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.9-H1** (design): when an improvement could take more than one double-sided location disc, the player chooses only if the candidates' other faces differ; otherwise the lowest-numbered disc is taken. Spec: `improve.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.9-H2** (design): a disc an improvement replaces returns to the general supply and may be taken again; a starting disc's plain back counts as an other face of its own for R-3.9-H1; with no disc of the needed location left the improvement is refused. Rejected: removing replaced discs from the game. Spec: `improve.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.10-H1** (Q8): Explore draws 2 Royal Writs plus 1 per outpost or feudum ruled beyond the first outpost. Rejected: 1 plus outposts ruled. Spec: `explore.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.10-H2** (design): Shovels & Swords is decided after keeping from the first Explore draw. Rejected: declaring it with Explore. Spec: `explore.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.10-H3** (design): Shovels & Swords is refused when no writ would be left to draw once the unkept go under: the iron buys nothing. The unkept writs go under first, so a short deck can return them. Spec: `explore.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.11-H1** (Q9): favours are drawn at random from the haversack; only Inspect the Harvest lets a player choose them, holding a bead or not. Spec: `harvest.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.11-H2** (design): a haversack that runs short under R-1.1-H1 fills the farm first: the partial, then the favours, so a seat draws favours only past the chart's partial. With no good left for a favour none was taken, so no bead flips and Inspect asks nothing. Rejected: filling the favours first; flipping beads for favours never received. Spec: `harvest.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.12-H1** (rulebook, revised): Reinforcements is the seat's to collect or decline, as the rulebook reads ("you may also collect"). With a Knight pawn on the board and a marker to take, `Tax` names its answer (`collect`), and a Tax without one is refused; declined, Tax pays and the card is played for its action alone. With no Knight pawn, or no marker to take, nothing is asked and Tax still pays. Rejected: collecting it by itself. Spec: `tax.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.13-H1** (Q11): Conquer may not be a player's last card played in the round; a card that player plays later in the round, including the second card of a sequential turn, makes it not last. A Conquer left as a player's only card is played with its action forfeited. The hand picker warns when a hand puts Conquer at risk of being last. Spec: `conquer.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.14-H1** (Q12, amended at design): whenever a player has a piece attacked, or a subject or serf starved, and public state cannot rule out that they hold Defend (they have an unplayed card, have not played Defend this round, and for Royal Immunity hold a food), the game asks them whether to flip it, whatever the odds. A player without Defend answers "no" with the same control, so being asked reveals nothing (R-5). Rejected: asking only a holder of Defend, which reveals the card; asking only when it could change the result. Spec: `defend.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.14-H2** (design): a Royal Immunity seal may be reclaimed in the at-will windows of R-4.4-H1. Spec: `defend.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.14-H3** (design): Royal Immunity's food is a payment, so a wild tile may cover it (R-4.4-H1), and a seat with a tile that can bring a food is asked as a seat holding a food is. The answer comes off the seat's turn, so it spends only the tiles the food falls short by, each naming food: none when the seat holds a food, and none when it lets the Starve stand. Rejected: asking only a seat with a food in its goods, which takes away a tile's cover; spending any tile named, which lets a seat use tiles out of turn at any moment. Spec: `defend.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.14-H4** (design): Royal Immunity needs a seal from the pile; with the pile empty public state rules it out, so nobody is asked and the Starve resolves. Rejected: cancelling with no seal to place. Spec: `starve.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.14-H5** (design): Royal Immunity scores no vp: Defend is an either/or card, the ability replaces the regular action whose flip scores 1 vp, and the ability prints none. Rejected: scoring the card's printed 1 vp. Spec: `defend.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.15-H1** (design): Repeat and Déjà Vu may replay a card that was forfeited earlier in the round, since it was played. Rejected: only a card played for its action. Spec: `repeat.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.15-H2** (design): Déjà Vu takes only a card without x2, as the rulebook's Déjà Vu box (p. 15) reads; a card with x2 replays by Repeat. Rejected: any card but Conquer and Defend, as the player reference reads. Spec: `repeat.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.16-H1** (design): a feast on a pull that reaches its threshold scores the threshold's points and also draws its 2 Royal Writs, keeping 1; only reeves are excluded. Rejected: points only. Spec: `feast.spec.ts`.
- **R-3.16-H2** (design): a feast scores its +3 vp whatever the function reached, so a pull or push below its threshold scores the 3 alone, with no writ. Rejected: +3 only on a function that scores. Spec: `feast.spec.ts`.

### Mechanisms (R-4)

- **R-4.1-H1** (design): when a serf with a Royal Immunity seal adds a marker, the seal stays under that marker, which becomes the subject. Rejected: the seal going back to the player. Spec: `locationControl.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.2-H1** (design): the pin stops a pawn's steps and nothing else, so a pawn that stepped onto an opposing monster's space may still pilfer there, and a pinned pawn may still feast on its space (R-3.16.3; the rulebook bars the monster, not the pinned pawn, from feasts). Rejected: reading "can only migrate, conquer or wait" as barring the pilfer or the feast, which the rulebook's pin sentence, about leaving the space, doesn't reach. Spec: `move.spec.ts`, `feast.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.2-H2** (rulebook, revised): an abandoned vessel is boarded only on a later turn, as the rulebook reads ("on a future turn"). A vessel left on a space during a turn (a step by road or by another vessel, a pawn migrating off, a pawn conquered) can't be boarded by any pawn until that turn ends, the second card of a sequential turn included; a vessel abandoned before the turn may be boarded beside it. The turn keeps where each vessel was left. Rejected: letting a later step of the same Move board a vessel an earlier step left. Spec: `move.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.2-H3** (design): `PlaceVessel`'s "own turn" covers the at-will windows before a card and over the writ limit on the seat's own turn, not hand selection or nourishing. Rejected: any at-will window, since those two fall outside the seat's turn. Spec: `atWill.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.3-H1** (Q20): when an improvement's tile is prohibited, the improving player chooses which stack to take an earlier-epoch tile from, among those that have one on top. Spec: `regionTiles.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.3-H2** (rulebook, FAQ): a wild tile becomes a good taken from the haversack, so it names a good the haversack holds; with none of that good left it can't be spent for it. The rulebook says resources are limited and a shortage may weaken or prevent a guild action, and the FAQ says every game component is limited to what the supply holds. Rejected: discarding the tile for nothing under R-1.1-H1. Spec: `payments.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.3-H3** (design): a payment with an at-will window before it is covered by `SpendRegionTile` in that window: `Improve`, `TendLandscape`, Déjà Vu's saltpeter, Feast's barrel sulfur, `FinaliseHand`'s pouch good. A payment no window precedes carries its own `wildTiles`: `CommitAttack`, `AnswerStarve`, and Shovels & Swords' iron in `KeepWrits`, decided after seeing the draw. `PlayMandate`, `Migrate` and `Nourish` also carry them, so one press pays. Rejected: a `wildTiles` field on every paying action. Spec: `atWill.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H1** (Q13): Royal Writ mandates and wild region tiles may be used on the player's own turns, during hand selection and nourishing, and (weapons) during the player's own attack; a wild tile is also offered whenever a payment would otherwise fall short. Rejected: out of turn at any moment. Spec: `royalWrits.spec.ts`, `regionTiles.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H2** (design): a Royal Writ draw larger than the deck draws what is there and keeps the stated number, or all drawn if fewer; an empty deck draws nothing. Spec: `royalWrits.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H3** (design): unkept writs go under the deck in the order drawn. Rejected: the drawer orders them. Spec: `royalWrits.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H4** (Q29, revised): mandate 6's Alchemist reward is any vessel, as the rulebook's writ summary reads; the seat names the vessel when it plays the mandate, and each vessel is offered, dimmed while the general supply has none (R-4.4-H7). The card's icon shows a ship. Rejected: only the ship the icon shows. Spec: `mandates.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H5** (rulebook): a seat may discard a writ it holds at any time until the game ends, on its turn or not, as the rulebook's hand limit reads. `DiscardWrit` is a sequenced out-of-turn Action that every state takes, and play stays where it was; it is not one of the at-will windows of R-4.4-H1. In a hotseat game the screen shows the seat play waits on, so each other seat holding a writ has a chip there that opens its own writs. Spec: `royalWrits.spec.ts`, `turnOrder.spec.ts`, `atWillDraft.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H6** (rulebook, revised): a charter is sealed only at the end of the game, as the rulebook reads; no Action seals one during play. Final scoring first puts each held king's seal on one held charter, the best-paying first, then scores the rows. A held seal scores nothing else, so this is the seat's best placement and nothing is asked. Rejected: sealing during play; asking each seat at the end which charters to seal. Spec: `scoring.spec.ts`, `royalWrits.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H7** (design): a mandate whose reward is a vessel needs one of its type in the general supply, as R-4.6-H8 does for a purchase. Any other reward pays what remains under R-1.1-H1, and the played mandate still goes under. It is a barrier only if goods were in fact drawn or a seal in fact flipped. Spec: `royalWrits.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H8** (FAQ): a discarded writ goes to the bottom of the deck face down, as the FAQ's easily missed rules read. Only the discarder knows which writ it was and where it lies; the table sees that a writ went under and the slot it took. Rejected: showing the discard to all. Spec: `royalWrits.spec.ts`, `actionProjection.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.4-H9** (rulebook): a seat may not hold more than 3 Royal Writs at any time, as the rulebook's hand limit reads. Whenever a step leaves a seat over the limit, play waits while it discards down, on its turn or not: the turn player first, then the others in turn order. The turn player may instead play a mandate (R-4.4-H1); a seat off its turn may only discard. The turn stays where it was. Rejected: checking the limit only when a turn ends. Spec: `turnOrder.spec.ts`, `engineWalk.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.5-H1** (Q18): after anything changes status stars, each guild's ranks are recomputed from the new totals; a current holder keeps a space against a tie. Nourishing is one such change (R-4.10.1): ranks are recomputed once, from every seat's new totals, on the Action that ends it, `NourishPawns` when no seat is asked or else the last `Nourish`; an influence mandate an asked seat plays at will meanwhile (R-4.4-H1) waits for that recompute too. Rejected: applying bumps player by player, or seat by seat as each is fed. Spec: `guildStatus.spec.ts`, `nourish.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.5-H2** (design): when two players who hold no rank tie for a vacant one, the acting player takes it first, then the others in turn order from them. Rejected: leaving it vacant until one has more stars. Spec: `guildStatus.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.5-H3** (design): a player owed membership of several guilds with too few markers in supply takes them in ring order from the Farmer, without being asked; a marker returning is placed the same way. Rejected: asking the player, a stop in a rule that has none. Spec: `guildStatus.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H1** (Q14), _provisional_: the Noble trade sells king's seals from the guild's scrolls; with no seal there, there is nothing to buy. Rejected: from the general supply. Spec: `guildTrade.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H2** (Q15): the hidden-bead draw (Monk trade, Farmer pull, Monk push) picks one of the highest- and lowest-valued beads at random; no opponent arranges them. Listed in `known-deviations.md`. Spec: `beads.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H3** (Q16), _provisional_: the apprentice's share from the Farmer, Merchant and Alchemist guilds goes to the church coffer; from the Knight, Noble and Monk guilds, to the farmer's purse. Spec: `guildTrade.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H4** (Q17): the krud barrel prices (1st, 2nd, 3rd) count barrels within one action, so a Knight pull's barrel always costs the first price. Rejected: counting barrels across the game. Spec: `alchemist.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H5** (Q19): a push or pull moves as many of the stated items as fit, at least one, and scores the level it reaches. Rejected: unavailable unless all fit. Spec: `guildFunctions.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H6**: when a Farmer trade passes the barn's limit, the spill is picked at random from the goods sent, up to the chicken beads' sum and the surplus; then the barn fills from what remains in the order of `Goods`, and the rest goes to the haversack. Any spill is a barrier; no seat is asked. Rejected: filling the barn first, or letting a seat choose what spills, since the rule asks for a random spill and leaves the rest open. Spec: `guildTrade.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H7** (design): a good a Merchant push or Alchemist pull takes from the market leaves its bin's highest filled space, as a purchase does, so the bin stays filled from the left. The rulebook says only which space a purchase takes. Spec: `merchant.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H8** (design): a krud barrel can be invented while any Knight row has an unfilled section, even if the general supply has no marker for that row; the row takes what the supply has. A vessel needs one of its type in the general supply. Spec: `alchemist.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H9** (design): each marker moved from a Knight row to the scrolls (Knight push, Noble pull) needs its own row, an empty scroll space and a seal in the general supply, so all three limit how many can move (R-4.6-H5); the actor picks the rows and the scroll spaces. Spec: `knight.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.6-H10** (design): a monster with no space to deploy to is not offered by the Knight trade, and the seat keeps its markers. Rejected: taming it with no space to stand on. Spec: `guildTrade.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.7-H1** (Q24, revised): a reeve scores on every push and every pull, threshold reached or not, as the rulebook's Reeves box reads: one already there scores, or one may be placed and scores. Points and writs still need a threshold (R-4.6.1). A trade scores no reeve; a Feast scores none (R-3.16.3). Rejected: reeves score only when the push or pull reaches a threshold, as the unofficial FAQ reads. Spec: `reeves.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.8-H1** (Q10): every piece the attacker and the defender have on the space joins the combat. Rejected: the attacker choosing which of theirs join. Spec: `combat.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.8-H2** (design): a conquered feudum is replaced by the lowest outpost disc in the general supply, unasked, and the feudum disc returns to the supply; with no outpost disc left, the feudum's own disc stays and counts as the outpost. Rejected: asking the attacker which disc, a choice with no effect until a later improvement replaces it anyway. Spec: `combat.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.8-H3** (design): a Royal Immunity seal under a marker leaving a conquered feudum, or under a serf who becomes its ruler, goes back to the marker's owner. Rejected: returning it to the pile. Spec: `combat.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.8-H4** (design): a conquered pawn's vessel stays on the space and its wine sulfur goes to the haversack, as for a pawn migrating off (R-3.6-H1). Spec: `combat.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.8-H5** (design): a Conquer that asks nobody resolves at once only when public state shows the attacker can add nothing: no saltpeter, no tile for one, and no unsealed writ. Otherwise the attacker is asked, so the prompt says nothing about what their writs are. The same test applies once the defender has answered: with nothing to add, `AnswerAttack` resolves the combat. Specs: `conquer.spec.ts`, `handlers.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.8-H6** (design): a wild tile may cover the saltpeter of a commitment as well as a weapon's turn-in (R-4.4-H1). Spec: `combat.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.9-H1** (Q21): after each successful Conquer or Starve the People the player is asked whether to place a military service disc. Spec: `militaryService.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.9-H2** (design): military service is per player: each vassal covers the track's three spaces with their own discs, several players' discs sharing a space, and disloyalty counts the vassal's own uncovered spaces. Rejected: one shared track, first come first served. Spec: `militaryService.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.10-H1** (official FAQ): a sulfur a region tile brings is a sulfur gained, so the seat chooses its supply or its wine barrel (at most 3). A tile can therefore bring a sulfur into the barrel just before a Feast. Spec: `atWill.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.10-H2** (design): every sulfur a seat's input can name goes where it names, through `sulfurToBarrel` (`SpendRegionTile`, a tile in any `wildTiles`, a pilfer, starting goods, a Merchant trade, `ChooseFavours`, a sulfur mine the seat collects). A sulfur the input can't name goes to the supply: one drawn at random (a mandate's goods, the favours of a harvest taken unseen), since the seat doesn't yet know what it draws, and the ruler's share of a collected sulfur mine, since the input is the serf's. Rejected: asking the seat after each such gain. Specs: `sulfurGains.spec.ts`, `harvest.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.10-H3** (official FAQ): a seat feeds every pawn its food and barrel can, food or wine as it likes; a pawn goes unfed only for want of both, and which one is the seat's choice. Spending a tile for food or wine is optional. A pawn holding a sulfur is fed by it and can't be named. Rejected: leaving a pawn unfed by choice. Spec: `nourish.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.10-H4** (design): nourishing waits, in turn order, on each seat with pawns to feed that has a feeding choice or may play at will in it (R-4.4-H1): one holding an unsealed writ, a region tile while the haversack holds a good, or a Royal Immunity seal to reclaim. The test reads only public counts (R-5.1), so a seat whose writs are all charters or unaffordable mandates is still asked; it sends its feeding. Every other seat is fed by the game. Rejected: feeding a seat with no feeding choice at once, which closed its at-will window unasked; asking only a seat with a playable mandate, which told the table it held one. Spec: `handlers.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.11-H1** (Q6): the epoch marker, and with it which region tiles are legal, changes only at step 4 of the round, even when an improvement met the condition earlier. Rejected: changing the moment the condition is met. Spec: `epochs.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.11-H2** (design): a tile the progress die removes leaves the game with its landscape shown, as a spent tile's is: the roll is a barrier either way, and the vault holds only tiles still in a stack. Rejected: keeping it hidden for the rest of the game. Spec: `progressDie.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.12-H1** (design): when a landscape's resource runs short at a dawn, the fill goes seat by seat in turn order, each seat's tended landscapes in space order, and untended landscapes after every seat's, in space order. Rejected: placing untended ones by their ruler's seat, since an untended landscape has no serf to serve and the rule's turn order is about the serfs who collect. Spec: `dawn.spec.ts`.
- **R-4.12-H2** (design): at the third dawn a guild's refill covers the spaces setup filled and adds nothing beyond them. A setup chicken that already holds a bead is covered: no bead is added, and an empty setup chicken takes one even when another chicken holds a bead. The rosary has no spaces, so it refills to setup's count; the vessel space likewise. The refill runs in setup's order (market, carts, vessels, Knight rows, scrolls, rosary, chicken, barn), so the barn's random goods come last, and each runs out as R-1.1-H1 says. Rejected: counting beads across the chickens, since the rule speaks of the spaces indicated at setup. Spec: `dawn.spec.ts`.

### Information (R-5)

- **R-5.4-H1** (design): the haversack's contents are shown as counts of each good, since every good outside it is public and its contents follow from public state. Spec: `visibility.spec.ts`.
- **R-5-H1** (Q22): king's seals red side up keep their bead values hidden from everyone, their holder included, until flipped; the supply of seals is a hidden shuffled pile, and a seal returning to it goes in red side up and the pile is reshuffled. Spec: `visibility.spec.ts`.

### Game end (R-6)

- **R-6.2-H1** (design), _provisional_: a kingdom charter prints 3/5/7/9 vp for 1 to 4 locations ruled on its spaces; 5 or more score 9. Spec: `charters.spec.ts`.
- **R-6.2-H2** (design): the sulfur-barrel charter counts the sulfur in the barrel at final scoring. A sulfur standing on a wine-fed pawn (R-4.10.2) has left the barrel and doesn't count. Rejected: counting it as barrel sulfur, against the card's "in barrel". Spec: `charters.spec.ts`.
- **R-6.1-H1** (Q7): when the condition for epoch V is met during a round (R-4.11.2), the round's remaining cards are played, pawns are nourished, the die is not rolled, epoch V's dawn is scored, then final scoring. Spec: `gameEnd.spec.ts`.
- **R-6.1-H2** (design): final scoring replaces the round's end at V, as the rulebook's round summary reads (step 5: if epoch V was triggered, final scoring instead). The marker does not pass, committed pouch goods stay unpaid, a drunk pawn keeps its sulfur and the played cards stay; none of it moves a score or the barrel tie-break (R-6.3). Rejected: ending the round before final scoring. Spec: `gameEnd.spec.ts`, `dawn.spec.ts`.

## Rules of the implementation

- **R-X.1:** every choice arrives as explicit input from the player it belongs to; the engine infers none.
- **R-X.2** (Q23): undo stops at an action that revealed information: any haversack draw, any Royal Writ draw, the progress die, a seal flipped to a bead, the hidden-bead draw, a finalised hand, and any action that passes a decision to another player (an attack that may be defended, a starve that may be resisted). The list is complete: playing a card, mandate or weapon from a hidden hand, or discarding a writ, is not a barrier, since its player learns nothing (design).
- **R-X.3:** a game keeps the rules of the revision it was created under (`feudumRevision`, set at initialization). Revision 1 is the first release.
