# Feudum open questions

Every place the digest is silent, ambiguous or self-contradictory, and every place two readings would make different code. Each has a recommended ruling. Once answered, each becomes an `R-x.y-H<n>` entry in `rulings.md` and is struck from here.

## How to read a question

- **Q<n> <short title>** (R-x.y): the question.
    - _Readings:_ the two or more ways the rule could go, and what each changes in play.
    - _Sources:_ what the rulebook, FAQ and designer each say.
    - _Recommendation:_ the reading to take and why, or "provisional" when only the publisher can settle it.

## Setup

- **Q1 Scope: advanced only, or a basic-game option?** (R-3.4)
    - _Readings:_ (a) advanced rules always; (b) a table option to play the basic game (no special abilities).
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook presents both; the designer calls the advanced rules the game as intended and the basic rules a warm-up (D 2018-05-13); players note the basic game cannot clear the chickens, since only Starve the People removes beads, so Farmer and Monk functions clog.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a) advanced only for 0.1.0; a basic option can come later as a variant.

- **Q2 Player counts and dummy rows** (R-1.4.4)
    - _Readings:_ with 2–3 players, (a) add dummy Knight rows until there are exactly 4; (b) add dummy rows for every unused colour (5 rows).
    - _Sources:_ rulebook: "add 'dummy' rows so there are at least 4 colors in play"; players used 4; dummy rows act as players' rows for krud and pushes but nobody trades for them (D 2018-05-15).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a) exactly 4 rows; 2–5 players.

- **Q3 Shillings: limited or unlimited?** (R-1.1)
    - _Readings:_ (a) limited to the 78 shillings in the box (50 coins), a payment from an empty supply paying what is left; (b) unlimited, as many digital games treat money.
    - _Sources:_ every component is limited (RB p. 17; FAQ; D 2018-05-15: no substitutes); nothing singles out money.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a) limited, to follow the rule; the history shows a short payment when it happens.

## Turn structure

- **Q4 Choosing hands: simultaneous or in turn order?** (R-3.1)
    - _Readings:_ (a) everyone chooses at once, but a player may not finalise until every earlier player in turn order has; each pouch good becomes public when its owner finalises; (b) fully simultaneous, pouch goods revealed together.
    - _Sources:_ "Long live the king": a player may wait to see earlier players' pouch goods; a later player may demand an earlier one finalise first (D 2017-10-21).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). It keeps the right to wait without anyone having to ask, and every player can still pick cards at the same time.

- **Q5 A player who has run out of cards** (R-3.2)
    - _Recommendation:_ skipped until everyone has played all chosen cards. The rules are silent, and nothing else works.

- **Q6 When the epoch changes after a mid-round trigger** (R-4.11.2)
    - _Readings:_ an Improve can remove tiles mid-round and expose the next epoch's condition. (a) The epoch marker, and with it which tiles are legal, changes only at step 4 of the round; (b) it changes at once.
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook checks at step 4 after the die; designer: for epoch V, finish the round first (D 2018-05-13); players: the check happens at one point (2018-03-18).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), for every epoch.

- **Q7 Finishing the round after epoch V is triggered** (R-6.1)
    - _Readings:_ after the cards, (a) nourish, skip the die, score dawn V, final scoring; (b) also roll the die.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The die can no longer matter; nourishing still can (pawns lost before final scoring change guild ranks).

## Actions

- **Q8 Explore: how many writs?** (R-3.10.1)
    - _Readings:_ (a) 2 + 1 per additional outpost or feudum ruled (rulebook); (b) 1 + outposts ruled (designer as relayed by a player, 2017-01-27).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), the printed rule. (b) is a relayed answer and ignores feudums.

- **Q9 Harvest favours with a rosary bead** (R-3.11.2)
    - _Readings:_ without a Farmer pawn, favours are (a) drawn at random; (b) chosen when you hold a bead (a player's reading).
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook draws favours at random and gives the choice only to Inspect the Harvest.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a).

- **Q10 Which pieces join an attack or a defence** (R-4.8.2)
    - _Readings:_ (a) all of the attacker's and the defender's pieces on the space count; (b) the attacker picks which of theirs join.
    - _Sources:_ "Players may attack or defend with multiple pawns"; designer: all the defender's pieces there count (D 2018-08-27).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), for both sides. Leaving one out never helps.

- **Q11 Conquer as the last card** (R-3.13.1)
    - _Readings:_ when Conquer is the only card a player has left, (a) it is played with its action forfeited; (b) the player is warned at selection and may not choose a hand that forces it (it can still happen through other players' extra actions).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), plus a warning in the card picker. Conquer counts as "last" if no card of that player follows it in the round; a second card in the same sequential turn counts as following.

- **Q12 Defend and Royal Immunity prompts** (R-3.14)
    - _Readings:_ when a player holding an unplayed Defend is attacked or starved, (a) the game always stops to ask them; (b) only when the card could change the outcome.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The flip also scores 1 vp, and asking only sometimes would leak information about the attack.

- **Q13 When mandates and wild tiles may be used** (R-4.3.3, R-4.4.2)
    - _Readings:_ "at any time" means (a) on your own turns, during hand selection, during nourishing, and weapons during your own attack; (b) also out of turn whenever anything happens.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). Those are the windows where a mandate or tile can change anything. Spending a tile is also offered whenever a payment would otherwise fall short (feasts included, as the FAQ allows).

## Guilds

- **Q14 Where bought king's seals come from** (R-4.6.3)
    - _Readings:_ Noble trade sells seals (a) from the scrolls in the guild; (b) from the general supply.
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook keeps seals "near the Noble Guild" as supply; designer-approved (2025-05-28): "items come from the guild; if the guild is empty there is nothing to buy". Every other trade sells from its guild (Merchant goods, Alchemist vessels, Knight rows, Monk beads).
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), from the scrolls, consistent with the other five guilds and the 2025 answer. Provisional: it changes the Noble economy.

- **Q15 The hidden-bead draw** (R-4.6.3, R-4.6.4)
    - _Readings:_ an opponent hides the high and low beads, one in each hand, and the actor picks. (a) The actor picks an opponent, who secretly arranges the beads; the actor then picks a hand. (b) The game picks one of the two at random.
    - _Recommendation:_ (b), listed in `known-deviations.md`. The opponent's arrangement is a guessing game with nothing to go on, so it is a coin flip either way; (a) would stop the game for a second player three times as often as any other interaction.

- **Q16 Apprentice share: purse or coffer?** (R-4.6.2)
    - _Readings:_ "opposite side of the board" means (a) the three left-edge guilds (Farmer, Merchant, Alchemist) pay the church coffer and the three right-edge guilds (Knight, Noble, Monk) the farmer's purse; (b) something else printed per guild that the pictures do not show.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). Provisional until a board close-up confirms it.

- **Q17 Krud barrel costs** (R-4.6.5)
    - _Readings:_ the 1st/2nd/3rd barrel prices count (a) within one action; (b) across the game.
    - _Sources:_ "the first krud barrel will always cost 3 goods".
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), so a Knight pull (one barrel) always pays the first price.

- **Q18 Guild status changes in one event** (R-4.5.4)
    - _Readings:_ when one event changes several players' stars (a conquered feudum, a pawn migrating off), (a) re-rank from scratch by the new totals, keeping the current holders where tied; (b) apply bumps one player at a time in turn order.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). It gives the same result whatever order the code visits players in.

- **Q19 Merchant "fewer if impossible" and partial pushes** (R-4.6.1)
    - _Readings:_ when only some of the stated goods fit, (a) move what fits and score what was completed; (b) the function is unavailable.
    - _Sources:_ designer-confirmed (2020-02-05, 2025-05-28): move as many as you can, score the level reached; at least one must move.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a).

- **Q20 Prohibited region tile: which stack?** (R-4.3.2)
    - _Recommendation:_ the improving player chooses among stacks whose top tile is from an earlier epoch than the current one.

## Combat and service

- **Q21 Military discs are optional** (R-4.9.1)
    - _Recommendation:_ after each successful Conquer or Starve, ask the player whether to place a disc, since discs are shared with reeves and the epic voyage (D 2017-10-21 treats the voyage disc as optional the same way).

## Information and undo

- **Q22 Seal values** (R-5.4)
    - _Readings:_ seals red side up (supply, scrolls, players' supplies) have (a) values hidden from everyone until flipped to beads; (b) values visible to their holder.
    - _Sources:_ the Knight push places seals "without peeking"; nothing else is said.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a). The general supply of seals is a shuffled hidden pile; a seal returning to the supply goes in red side up and the pile is reshuffled.

- **Q23 Undo boundaries**
    - _Recommendation:_ these reveal information and end Undo: any haversack draw (harvest, farmer trade, spill-over, mandates, replenishment, setup), any Royal Writ draw, the progress die, a seal flipped to a bead, the hidden-bead draw, finalising a hand ("Long live the king"), and any action that hands the decision to another player (an attack the defender may answer, a starve). Everything else may be undone within the player's turn.

## Game end and scoring

- **Q24 Reeve points when a push or pull scores nothing** (R-4.7)
    - _Readings:_ (a) reeves score only when the push or pull reaches a threshold; (b) whenever it is performed.
    - _Sources:_ the erratum's wording does not say; the designer's earlier Kickstarter answer and the FAQ say an unsuccessful function scores nothing, reeves included.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a).

## Map

- **Q25 Route segments read only from a community aid** (R-1.2.2)
    - Roads, ship, submersible and flying-machine routes were read from a fan-made map aid; the publisher board picture (1900 × 750) is too small to confirm them. Unconfirmed in particular: whether forest-2–forest-3 and badlands-1–forest-3 are separate roads; whether the mountain road reaches badlands-4; the submersible segments near desert-1 and desert-2; the whole flying-machine chain. The designer said three spaces are reachable by exactly one vessel type each, and that the north mountain space is not a port (D 2018-05-13).
    - _Recommendation:_ provisional: build from the aid, check against a clear photo of the physical board before the map ticket closes. See "Unread values".
