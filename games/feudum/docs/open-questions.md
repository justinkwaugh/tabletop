# Feudum open questions

Every place the digest is silent, ambiguous or self-contradictory, and every place two readings would make different code. Each has a recommended ruling. Once answered, each becomes an `R-x.y-H<n>` entry in `rulings.md` and is struck from here.

## How to read a question

- **Q<n> <short title>** (R-x.y): the question.
    - _Readings:_ the two or more ways the rule could go, and what each changes in play.
    - _Sources:_ what the rulebook, FAQ and designer each say.
    - _Recommendation:_ the reading to take and why, or "provisional" when only the publisher can settle it.

## Open

- **Q30 Asking a seat with no pawn to feed** (R-4.10.1, R-4.4): should a seat with no pawn to feed, but able to act at will (an unsealed writ, a region tile while the haversack holds a good, or a Royal Immunity seal to reclaim), be asked during nourishing, extending R-4.4-H1's "during nourishing" to it?
    - _Readings:_ (a) not asked: nourishing waits only on seats with pawns to feed (R-4.10-H4), so a seat with none has no at-will window in it; (b) asked as a seat with pawns is, so it may play a mandate, spend a tile or reclaim a seal before nourishing ends.
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook lets mandates and wild tiles be used at any time, and says nothing of nourishing for a seat with no pawn on the board.
    - _Recommendation:_ (b), as R-4.4-H1's "during nourishing" reads; it needs its own press for the seat to say it is done, and R-4.10-H4 reworded. Until the designer rules, the engine follows (a).

- **Q31 A kingdom charter past its printed values** (R-4.4.3, R-6.2): a kingdom charter prints 3/5/7/9 vp for 1 to 4 locations ruled on its spaces; what do 5 or more score?
    - _Readings:_ (a) 9, the top printed value; (b) more, continuing the printed steps of 2.
    - _Sources:_ the card and the rulebook print the four values and say nothing past them.
    - _Recommendation:_ provisional (a), as R-6.2-H1 records and the provisional registry holds; only the designer can settle it.
- **Q32 A score past 149** (R-6, R-1.2.5): the scoring track runs 0 to 149; how does a score past it count?
    - _Readings:_ (a) uncapped: the marker laps the track and the score keeps counting; (b) capped at 149.
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook prints the track and says nothing of a score that passes its end.
    - _Recommendation:_ (a), which the engine follows; the track's wrap is presentation. Until the designer rules, it stays open.

## Settled

Q1–Q25 (setup, turn structure, actions, guilds, combat, information, game end, map) were answered on 2026-11-01 by accepting every recommendation; each is now a ruling in `rulings.md`, citing its question number.

Q26–Q29 (region tile stacks, vessel routes, values printed nowhere, mandate 6's reward) were settled on 2026-11-06, by the same route.

Revised to suit an online table:

- Q4: every player finalises when they like and the pouch goods are revealed together once all have finalised, not in turn order with each pouch good public as it is finalised; the rulebook's later player waiting on an earlier pouch good is a known deviation (R-3.1-H1).

Answers since revised to follow the rulebook:

- Q24: a reeve scores on every push and every pull, as the rulebook's Reeves box reads, not only when it reaches a threshold (R-4.7-H1).
- Q18: nourishing is one change of status (R-4.10.1), so ranks are recomputed once, on the Action that ends it, from the new totals, not seat by seat as each is fed; an influence mandate played at will while seats are being fed waits for that recompute (R-4.5-H1).
- Q29: mandate 6's Alchemist reward is any vessel, as the rulebook's writ summary reads, not only the ship its icon shows (R-4.4-H4).

Three design rulings were revised the same way: Reinforcements is collected or declined, not automatic (R-3.12-H1); an abandoned vessel is boarded only on a later turn (R-4.2-H2); charters are sealed only at game end (R-4.4-H6).
