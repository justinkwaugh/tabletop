# Feudum open questions

Every place the digest is silent, ambiguous or self-contradictory, and every place two readings would make different code. Each has a recommended ruling. Once answered, each becomes an `R-x.y-H<n>` entry in `rulings.md` and is struck from here.

## How to read a question

- **Q<n> <short title>** (R-x.y): the question.
    - _Readings:_ the two or more ways the rule could go, and what each changes in play.
    - _Sources:_ what the rulebook, FAQ and designer each say.
    - _Recommendation:_ the reading to take and why, or "provisional" when only the publisher can settle it.

## Open: component data

Questions the component values raised once the Tabletopia edition's textures were read (`components.md`, source TT).

- **Q26 Region tile stacks** (R-1.4.3, R-4.3): no region is printed on either face of a region tile, and each epoch's six tiles mix the four landscapes 2/2/1/1 or 1/1/2/2.
    - _Readings:_ (a) each region's stack is fixed, one tile per epoch with a set landscape; (b) at setup each epoch's six tiles are shuffled, one into each region's stack, and a tile's landscape stays hidden until it is taken. (b) makes setup random and adds hidden information (the tile under each region's top).
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook stacks the tiles by epoch and is silent on shuffling; the Tabletopia edition keeps each epoch's tiles in one shuffled bag.
    - _Recommendation:_ (b), as the published digital edition does. It replaces the placeholder "fixed per tile, never shuffled".
- **Q27 Vessel routes** (R-1.2.2, R-4.2.2): the board draws roads, piers and ferries, but no ship, submersible or flying-machine routes.
    - _Readings:_ (a) the community map aid's route lists (R-1.2-H1, provisional); (b) vessels move between adjacent spaces across water (ship, submersible) or over any terrain (flying machine), as the rule text describes.
    - _Sources:_ the rulebook's vessel text; the Tabletopia board (no vessel routes); the community map aid.
    - _Recommendation:_ check the vessel rules against the rulebook before the map is built, and take (b) if the text supports it, amending R-1.2-H1.
- **Q28 Values printed nowhere** (R-1.4.3, R-4.6.2): copies of each feudum disc and of each double-sided disc pair (farm/town, outpost/farm, outpost/town), the rosary's capacity, the Farmer's chicken spaces, and which charity each guild pays.
    - _Readings:_ only a photo of the physical discs or a designer ruling settles them.
    - _Recommendation:_ keep provisional placeholders: 2 feudums per guild, the double-sided pairs split 4/3/3 (farm/town, outpost/farm, outpost/town), the rosary unlimited, 4 chicken spaces, and the R-4.6-H3 charity split.
- **Q29 Mandate 6's Alchemist reward** (R-4.4.2): the rulebook's writ summary reads "any vessel"; the card prints a ship icon.
    - _Readings:_ (a) any vessel; (b) a ship only.
    - _Recommendation:_ (b), the card as printed, unless the rulebook's own card picture shows otherwise.
