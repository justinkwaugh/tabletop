# 18MEX harness scenarios

Written by `games/18mex/src/scenarios.build.spec.ts` (run with `SCENARIOS=1`) from seeded bot
games, all for four players. The first seven keep the actions that led to them; the special
tiles and brown Mexico City positions lay tiles in code, so their history starts afresh.

- **Private auction**: the opening auction, no action taken. The first player buys the MCAR at
  $20 or bids on a later item (3.1).
- **First stock round**: every item sold; the MIR's owner holds a CHI share and the MNR's the
  NdM's president's certificate, its par set. NdM stock is not on sale (5.1).
- **Minors operate**: minor B's run in its first operating round, from Mazatlán to its seaport,
  $30 split half to its owner and half to its treasury (4.2, MI-2).
- **Ndm merger**: the first 5-train has been bought; the first player offered the merger
  decides whether to merge one of their companies into the NdM (5.3).
- **Upgrade mexico city**: the NdM's track step in phase 5 with the green Mexico City double tile
  among its lays; tapping Mexico City or Puebla chooses the pair, laid as one for Puebla's $60
  (4.4.1(g), TR-4).
- **Upgrade mexico city to brown**: the NdM's track step with the brown Mexico City double tiles,
  485 (three stations) or 486 (four), among its lays, for the $40 printed on Puebla's green
  half (Table IV, TR-4, TR-5).
- **Late game**: the 4D phase, in a stock round.
- **Special tiles**: Copper Canyon, the Mazatlán, Tampico and Veracruz yellow tiles and the
  green Mexico City double tile laid over the first stock round, to see them drawn.
- **Brown mexico city**: the brown Mexico City double tile (485) over the late game, with its
  two separate lanes toward Puebla and Toluca joined to the city (TR-5).
