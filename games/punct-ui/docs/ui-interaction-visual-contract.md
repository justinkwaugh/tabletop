# UI Interaction Visual Contract

PÜNCT's board is one SVG seen from the near side of the table. A turn either brings a piece in from the reserve or moves one already in play; either way the piece hovers where the player has put it until it is dropped.

## Coexistence and precedence

The session's derivations decide every choice; the board reads them.

- **Placing and moving on one turn.** Both offers show together: a tap on a choosable space places, a tap on a movable piece picks it up. Picking a piece up replaces a staged new piece; choosing a kind lets go of a piece being moved. A new kind keeps the chosen space only if it fits there; either way the hovering piece is a new one of that kind, dropping in where it is put, and never keeps the old kind's shape.
- **Hovering piece and destination marks.** The marks under the hovering piece are hidden; the others stay, dimmed, so another space can be chosen. Choosing another space moves the hovering piece there.
- **Hovering piece and other pieces.** It is drawn after whatever stands on its spaces below it, by every space it sweeps over as it turns; lifted clear (a turn blocked both ways, a flight) it is drawn over everything.
- **A move landing and taps.** From the moment a move, Undo or history step starts until its state lands, taps, turns, letting go, Place, Drop and Undo of a choice do nothing; the marks fade with the step.
- **History and a staged piece.** Entering History, or any new state other than your own drop or your own Undo, clears the hovering piece at once, with no lift-off.
- **Turning the board and motion.** The 90° and Far side toggles land the move playing at once; a switch of view lands it and drops the rest of its replay. The staged piece's own motion carries on in the new view.
- **Viewers.** Only the player who made a choice sees it; anyone else viewing (the harness's Non-active view) sees none of it, and Undo passes it by.

History View, replay and silent restoration: History View shows no choices and no hover. A step with an action plays in full (a replayed move glides 0.5 s, a replayed placement drops with a bounce); a step without one (Undo, another player's Undo, a state-only history step, entering or leaving an exploration) moves every piece it changes at once, each on the step's timeline from 0, and ends within 0.18 s. Page loads, server syncs, edits and view switches show the board at rest.

## Render ownership

- **Draw order** (`board/drawOrder.ts`, `sceneOrder`): one order for the pieces, the hovering piece and the PÜNCT marker: over a shared space the lower first, otherwise of their nearest spaces in neighbouring columns the farther first. It is worked out again only when a piece or the hover's place in it changes, never at each frame.
- **Moving pieces** (`animators/piecePose.ts`, `MovingPiece.svelte`): the hovering piece and each piece in flight are drawn by a pose that writes `translate()`, opacity and, while it turns, the live body's paths straight to its nodes. No SVG motion on the board is GSAP's `matrix()`, which Firefox 140 misplaces under the board's view-scale zoom; pieces at rest move by `animators/svgPose.ts`. A piece shows its pre-rendered image whenever it lies a way one is drawn for, the live drawing kept under it until the image has loaded.
- **Choice marks** (`Board.svelte`): two-tone, a dark band under a cyan core. A movable piece's ring and the target rings on its top dots belong to that piece's drawing, so nearer pieces cover them; target rings on empty spaces lie on the board under every piece; click areas for destinations on pieces, and rings under the chosen piece itself, stand over everything. Each ring is its own node, faded on its own by the step that ends the choice, never as a board-sized group.
- **Level badges**: inside the piece's drawing after its marks, registered as one of its nodes, so they move with it.
- **Tap areas of pieces** (`pieceShape.ts`, `silhouetteOf`): a piece, at rest or moving, takes taps only over its silhouette, its side at every height and its top. Its images take none, as their empty corners overlap the spaces round it.
- **Tap off**: a `role="presentation"` wrapper round the board lets go on a click that reaches no choosable button.
- **Text**: coordinate labels and level badges sit on the alphabetic baseline lowered by 0.35 of the font size in user units, letter-spacing in px.
- **Looks** (`scripts/render-looks.mjs` from the `/looks` route): piece bodies, dishes and holes at 4 px per board unit; icons at 1.25 px per unit, 2.5 times their largest size on screen. Specks are drawn live over them, seeded by the piece's id.
