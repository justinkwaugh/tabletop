# UI Interaction Visual Contract

## 1. Visual Intents

| Intent        | Trigger               | What should be emphasized                                                                                                | What should be dimmed                | What must remain unaffected |
| ------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| place_piece   | The viewer is placing | Nothing: every empty space takes a piece, so none is marked                                                              | Nothing                              | Pieces on the board         |
| choose_stack  | The viewer may move   | Every stack with a legal move (its moulded rim recoloured cyan on the top face)                                          | Nothing                              | Other stacks                |
| choose_target | A stack is chosen     | The chosen stack (thicker rim), a dashed cyan rim, each dash outlined in black, on the top of every stack it can land on | Other movable stacks' marks (hidden) | Other stacks                |

## 2. Visual Primitives

- **Stack** (`StackShape.svelte`, sizes in `board/pieceGeometry.ts`, shape in `board/pieceShape.ts`, colors and specks in `board/pieceStyle.ts`, speck paths in `board/specks.ts`): donut pieces with a dished top seen from the near side of the table, nested with a dark seam between them; only the top piece shows its face. Each whole piece is an image of its colour, pre-rendered at 6.25 px per board unit (2.5 times the board's largest fit) by `scripts/render-looks.mjs` from the `/looks` page (`DiscLook.svelte`, `LookDefs.svelte`; rerun it after changing how a piece is drawn): its side a lathe of fine slices, gently shaded so white stays white, and its top bands from its height profile, each slope catching the light where it faces you and in shade where it faces away, fading smoothly round. Its own specks are drawn over it as one path per colour, clipped to the piece (`PieceDefs.svelte`); a thinned piece is a shaded band with its specks fitted inside it. The hole is open: down it show the inner walls of the pieces in turn, darker toward the sides, each at its true thickness with a thin seam between pieces, and below them the board once the stack is low enough. Past thirteen pieces a stack rises no higher and its bands thin out (a move that takes it past thirteen lands and bounces at the full height first, then shrinks over 0.3 s), except DVONN pieces, which keep most of their thickness so a buried one stays a clear red band. A stack of two or more carries its height on a badge, TZAAR's size: a white disc with a black number at the far right of the top piece; on the far left if a nearer stack covers that, and at the top's far middle, beyond the hole, if it covers both (`board/badgeSpot.ts`, chosen in `Board.svelte` from the stacks drawn after it; drawn by `HeightBadge.svelte` after the selection ring, over it, registered with the animator as the stack's `badge` node so it moves with the body); a stack in flight carries its own badge in its body, and the one it lands on shows the new height once it lands. The Height badges toggle (`heightBadges` title preference, on by default) hides them, except on stacks shrunk past the full height, whose height cannot be seen. Speckled like the real pieces on every surface (face, side, hole wall, thinned bands) at one density per area, seeded by the space each piece was placed on, spread evenly with no bare patches (best-candidate placement): on the side evenly round its curve; on the top from the hole out to the edge, each speck lit and squeezed as the surface under it, shrunk to keep inside the edge and out of the hole (left out if it would shrink too far). On the board seen from the far side the specks on a top turn half round with it, and the side and hole wall show their other half. Never carries interaction state.
- **Shadows** (`Board.svelte`): a radial-gradient ellipse under each stack, inside the stack's animated group, so it moves and fades with it. Where the board shows down a stack's hole (stacks of one to three, `throughLens` in `board/pieceShape.ts`), light falls straight in: a clip from `PieceDefs.svelte` keeps the shadows out of the hole, which takes `HOLE_SHADE` (0.4) of their shade instead. The far wall between a hole's pieces takes the same share in every stack, so its gaps are as light in a tall stack as in a short one; a piece in the air casts a ring. The player cards' tallies do the same. No filters on pieces.
- **Board** (`Board.svelte`, `graphBoard` title preference, `BoardStyleToggle` showing the board a press switches to): the old edition's pale sand (grain from `board/sandTexture.ts`, rendered once by `scripts/render-sand.mjs` to `images/sand.webp` at the sand's own size, `BoardView.sandBox`, and placed on it without a browser's fitting, clipped to the rounded outline from `board/geometry.ts`) pressed with a ring at every space, by default; or the newer graph board, black lines along the three directions through every space with short whiskers, on a pale area that ends at the outer lines.
- **Far side** (`turned` title preference, a toggle beside the board style's): the board turned half round, seen from the far side of the table, so what a tall stack hides comes in front of it. The board is the same shape that way round, so only the spaces' places change (`BoardView.plan`, `viewFor`); labels move with their columns, and each view's frame holds both ways round so the board keeps its size.
- **Stack limit** (`shrinkStacks` title preference, off by default, a toggle beside Far side): stacks past 13 pieces shrink to that height, thinning their middles; off, every stack stands whole (`session.pieceModel` with no cap), for players who would rather turn the board to see past a tall one. The frame keeps room above the far row for a whole stack of 30 (`FRAMED_HEIGHT`) in both modes, so the board does not move when the limit is switched; fitted by width on phones and most desktops, the board sits lower in the spare room below it rather than shrinking.
- **Marks** (`Board.svelte`, drawn in each stack's group after the stack but outside its moving group, so nearer stacks cover them as they cover pieces; the animator fades every mark with the move): two-tone ellipses, a dark band under a cyan core (`--dvonn-mark`), drawn on the top face's moulded rim at the stack's current height, so a mark never reaches a neighbour. Inputs `session.movable`, `session.chosenFrom`, `session.destinations`.
- **Coordinate labels** (`board/coordinates.ts`): BoardSpace style; each row's number before its first space on the left, each diagonal column's letter beyond its bottom space, both on the table outside the board. Centred by `dy` on the alphabetic baseline with letter-spacing in user units, and the height badge's digits by a `dy` in user units, as WebKit misplaces `dominant-baseline` and Safari em lengths under the board's zoom.
- **Board geometry** (`board/boardLines.ts`, `board/marks.ts`): the graph board's lines, where a mark, a badge and a tap target sit on a stack of a given height.

## 4. Shared State Contracts

- **Animation scope**: the board animates only `own-action`, `other-action`, `undo`, `history` and `exploration` transitions; page loads, server syncs and view switches show the board at rest. A step with an action plays it in full, a history replay included: an action played says what moved and what was cut off (its metadata), so a landed stack that is itself cut off hops, lands and floats away.
- **Steps without an action** (Undo, another player's Undo, a state-only history step or jump, exploration): the animator reads the change from the two boards by each piece's identity, the space it was placed on (`board/boardDiff.ts`), however many moves lie between them: every run of pieces that now tops a stack elsewhere hops there, in a straight line when a jump carries it between spaces off one line; stacks gone entirely float away and stacks back drop in. Both of a step's motions are kept and overlap (`QUICK_PHASE`): stacks dropping back in go with the hops home, stacks cut off float away as the hops end. Everything ends within 0.18 s, plus at most 0.01 s waiting for the board to draw; `tests/undo-timing.spec.ts` holds each step to 0.2 s.
- **Fliers** (`BoardAnimator.fliers`, one per moving run, keyed by its bottom piece): a stack in flight is drawn by its flier, with its badge, and left out of the board's stacks until it lands. Pieces in front cover a flier while it is low only when it is the one flier (`occluders`).
- **Moving SVG** (`animators/svgPose.ts`): every moving node tweens a plain pose (`x`, `y`, `scale`) that writes `translate()` and `scale()` as it is set; opacity stays on the node. GSAP writes SVG transforms as `matrix()`, whose offset Firefox 140 leaves out of the board's view-scale zoom; `tests/motion.spec.ts` checks no board transform is a `matrix()` through a move that cuts off, Undo, history steps and a shrink.
- **Shrinking past the full height**: the stack lands whole, then thins a piece's height at a time over 0.3 s (`shrinking`, set at a few timeline points rather than every frame).
- **Hands** (`DvonnGameSession.piecesInHand`): a step that brings pieces onto the board sets the hands it leads to as it starts (`landHands`, until the state lands), so a piece dropping in is not counted in hand too.
- **Staged selection** (`selection.from`, a space key): manual only. Registered with the session's `localSelections`, which pops it on Undo and clears it whenever a new state is published. It belongs to the player who made it (`selectionOwner`): anyone else viewing, as the harness's Non-active view is, sees none of it and Undo passes it by. Tapping the chosen stack again, a tap on the board that misses every target (`letGo`, on a `role="presentation"` wrapper round the board), or Undo lets go of it. Taps on the choices do nothing while a move plays (`updatingVisibleState`).
- **Undo of your own move** (`willUndo` notes the move): `restagedMove` holds only while it is the move this Undo takes back (its index is the incoming state's action count) and the view stays with its mover; `beforeNewState` then chooses its stack again, and the next Undo lets go of it.
- **Stopping a move** (`DvonnGameSession.moveInterrupter`, set by `BoardAnimator`): `beforeNewState` abandons the move on `view-switch`, and the board style, coordinates, far side and stack limit toggles interrupt it, since its motion was laid out in the old board. Either kills its tweens and lands it at once; the animator tells an abandoned presentation's later steps by `presentingState`. A move's `afterAnimations` writes its board only while the state it began on is still shown.
- **Target lists**: derived from the displayed state; empty when the viewer cannot act, including in History View.

## 5. Forbidden Couplings

- Do not derive legality in components; read the session lists.
- Do not override `undo()`; register selections with `localSelections`.

## 8. Change Rules

When touching interactive visuals:

1. identify the intent being changed
2. identify the primitives involved
3. verify whether shared state contracts are being widened
4. update this document if a new interaction or coupling is introduced
