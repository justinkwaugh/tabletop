# UI Interaction Visual Contract

## 1. Visual Intents

| Intent         | Trigger                                                                          | What should be emphasized                                                                  | What should be dimmed                 | What must remain unaffected |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| choose_marble  | Acting player may place and more than one colour is available                    | Selectable pool chips (faint cyan border); the chosen chip (cyan border)                   | Colours with none left (faded marble) | Board rings                 |
| place_marble   | A colour is chosen (manually, or the only one available) and no marble is staged | Nothing on the rings, since every vacant ring takes a marble; hover preview of the marble  | Nothing                               | Pool counts, player trays   |
| remove_ring    | A marble is staged in its ring (`chosenPlace`)                                   | The rings free once it is placed (dashed cyan mark, whole while hovered)                   | Nothing                               | Marbles, ghost positions    |
| change_staged  | A marble is staged and another colour could be placed                            | The staged marble's ring (thick solid cyan mark)                                           | Nothing                               | Free rings keep their mark  |
| choose_jumper  | A capture is required and no jumper is chosen                                    | Marbles with a legal jump (solid cyan mark)                                                | Nothing                               | Pool (inactive)             |
| choose_landing | A jumper is chosen, or a capture chain continues                                 | The chosen jumper (thick solid cyan); its landing rings (dashed cyan, whole while hovered) | Other jumpers' marks, to 40%          | Pool (inactive)             |

## 2. Visual Primitives

- **Choice mark** (`Board.svelte`, `.mark`): a cyan ellipse (`--zertz-mark` #5ef0ff, as in every title) round the base of each ring with a role other than placing, without the dark band other titles lay under it, since every mark here lies on a black ring: solid for a marble that can jump, thicker for the chosen jumper and the staged marble, dashed for rings to land on or remove (a whole number of dashes round the ring), whole while hovered or focused; other jumpers fade to 40% once one is chosen. Drawn on its own layer above the rings so a ring pulled out leaves its outline in place. The `BoardAnimator` fades the whole outline layer on the move's timeline, for as long as the move animates (at least 0.28s; 0.18s without an action), so a removed ring's outline fades while the ring is pulled out. The layer is keyed on the displayed state, so each new position gets a fresh layer whose outlines fade in (0.12s). Inputs: the role from session targets, hover and keyboard focus. Must not encode game state beyond the role.
- **Placement preview** (`Board.svelte`, `.preview`): translucent marble on the hovered placement ring. Inputs: `chosenColor` and local hover. Must not appear for any other role.
- **Ghost position** (`Board.svelte`, `.ghost`): dashed outline where a ring has been removed, drawn under the rings so a ring sliding out uncovers it and one sliding back in covers it. Inputs: layout positions minus the drawn rings, plus the rings sliding (`animator.sliding`) until the state lands. Never interactive.
- **Pulled-out ring** (`BoardAnimator`, directions from `board/pullOut.ts`): a removed ring is pulled out of the board as the real ones are: it slides across the table a ring's width without brushing a ring that stays, as the rules let a free ring go: toward an open side (where no ring stands) whose two neighbouring sides are open too, or between two adjacent open sides, whichever faces most nearly away from the board's middle: along a diagonal where one is open, since straight up the screen read as lifting away, except in the middle column, whose end rings go straight up or down; it gathers speed (`power2.in`, 0.45s; 0.14s without an action) and fades over the last 60%, its shadow going with it. Rings that touch and go together (a group claimed by a placement) go as one. A ring whose removal cuts groups off first slides out of its place the way it is free to go among the rings still there (0.3s, `power2.inOut`; 0.06s without an action; in Claim by ring removal, B2 up-left), then leaves beside the group it cut off, which goes as one once its marbles have floated up, so it never crosses them. The ghost dashes mark where each stood. Undo slides the rings back in the way they went (`power2.out`, 0.14s, or two legs of 0.07s when a ring cut a group off, the group with the first leg and that ring back into its place in the second); the marbles settle back in from 0.14s to 0.2s, including, on your own Undo of a placement whose ring was claimed, the marble that is restaged there, which then stands staged. Another player's Undo and a step back through history read the move they take back by its index (`undoneAction`), so they slide back the same way. A marble Undo restages keeps the specks it had. Never interactive.
- **Coordinate labels** (`Board.svelte`, `.coordinate-labels`, from `board/coordinates.ts`): each column's bottom and top ring names below and above it, BoardSpace style (A1 under the bottom-left ring, A4 over the top-left). Drawn from the setup's full layout, so they stay put as rings are removed. Shown while the `showCoordinates` title preference is on (an `OverlayToggle` in the board overlay). Never interactive.
- **Board view** (`board/geometry.ts` `BoardView`): always seen from the side of the table (`TILT` 0.75), rings as hollow cones and marbles above them. The straight-down view and its Tilt toggle were removed; to bring them back, restore from commit `13ba7e438`.
- **Board toggles** (`OverlayToggle.svelte`, in `GameTable.svelte`'s overlay): a row of round chips at the board's bottom right, mirroring the zoom controls at the bottom left, in room the board's frame leaves below the bottom labels (no band is reserved above the board): A1 for the coordinate labels, then the auto-jump chip (below), then, last as in every GIPF-project title, two turning arrows for the far side, the board seen half a turn round (`viewOf(true)`), with each label still beyond its column's end. Each is the player's own title preference (`showCoordinates`, `turned`); the far side is off by default, coordinates on. Each chip's hover text says what a press does now.
- **Auto-jump chip** (a drawn robot head and a marble hopping over another, between A1 and the far side; the creation form names it 🤖↷●): not a preference but the player's own standing declaration in the game (`SetAutoJump`, out of turn and supersedable), so the server makes their jumps with one choice even while they are away. Pressed while `gameState.autoJumps(me)`. Shown only to a seated player while the game lasts, and never in a game created with auto-jump for both (`autoJumpForAll`). Either player may press it at any time, on their turn or not; it is disabled (half opacity) while viewing history. Pressing it repeatedly replaces the standing declaration rather than adding one, unless a jump has followed it. A declaration moves nothing on the board and has no history line (the animator skips any step whose rings and marbles are unchanged, including a declaration replaced in place, so history stays enabled), so a press that changes it shows "Auto-jump ON" or "Auto-jump OFF", read from the game once the action settles (a press that fails or is rejected shows none), over the chip row's right end for 2 s, in the chip's ink (`--zertz-ink` on, `--zertz-ink-quiet` off), fading in over 150 ms and out over 300 ms; a press during the last one replaces it. The hover text says whether it is on and what a press does.
- **Automatic jumps** (System `Capture` actions in the cascade of the action that left the jump): each plays as an ordinary jump step, one after the other, so an opponent's move answered by a chain shows the placement, then each jump, then the turn back with them. Undoing the move takes the chain back with it.
- **Water** (`app.css` `.zertz-water`): matte #0f527c. Full screen redraws the same layer. Never interactive.
- **Each-colour outline** (`PlayerTray.svelte`, `.trio`): rounded outline around the first `eachColor` slots of every tray row; lit (`.met`, the house orange `--zertz-target`, as is the active player's tray) once that colour reaches the each-colour target. Reflects captures only.
- **Shadows** (`Board.svelte`, `zertz-ring-shadow` and `zertz-marble-shadow` gradients from `board/softShadow.ts`): radial-gradient fills whose stops follow the edge a 2.5-unit Gaussian blur gives, rather than a blur filter, which is slow to paint on phones and can leave a removed shape's pixels on screen in WebKit. Rings and marbles above them stay sharp when zoomed.
- **Staged marble** (`StagedMarble.svelte`, motion from `animators/drop.ts`): the marble you are placing, standing in the ring you chose until you choose a ring to remove. It drops in as it is drawn, unless Undo took it back out there; taken back by the player (letting go, or Undo of the choice), it lifts out (0.18s), from wherever a drop still under way has it, as the lift-off stops the drop (the drop moves nodes inside the ones Svelte lifts off, so they never write the same transform); when the move goes in it steps aside without lifting and the board's own marble takes its place: at rest once the staged drop has landed, carrying on from where the staged drop has it while it is still falling, or coming to rest from mid-bounce (0.12s) (the animator reads the pose from the `StagedDrop` the marble registers). A placement sent the moment its ring is chosen (no ring to remove, or a placing that ends the game) drops in as anyone else's does. Keyed by colour, so a colour change lifts one out as the next drops in. When a published state, History or a switch of view or perspective clears the selection, it goes without lifting (`releasedByPlayer`, reset by `beforeNewState`, false in History View and for anyone but the selection's owner). Inputs: `chosenPlace`, `chosenColor`, `releasedByPlayer`, the animator's `placedIn` and `returned`.
- **Pool chip** (`PoolStrip.svelte`): marble and count; selectable and chosen states.
- **Ring** (`RingLook.svelte`): one image of a black plastic ring, a thin hollow cone with a faint soft sheen down the lit side, a darker crease at its base and a shaded lip round its hole, lit pixel by pixel (`board/pieceLight.ts`) at 6.25 px per board unit (2.5 times the largest size the board fits at; a little soft at full zoom) on the `/looks` dev page and saved to `src/lib/images/looks/` by `scripts/render-looks.mjs`, which also writes `board/lookUrls.ts` importing each file by name (never a glob, which fails in the bundle). The light is the viewer's, from the upper left, so the turned view shows the same image. Its hit area is the `.ring-hit` ellipse round its base. Drawn live as gradient paths, the rings looked flat and cost more to open. Never carries interaction state.
- **Marble** (`MarbleLook.svelte`): its colour's lit body image (saved with the ring), its own specks (white on black, black on grey) and one faint shine image over every marble. The specks lie on the sphere, seeded by the ring and the move that placed the marble (the seed the state carries, see Marble specks below; `board/marbleSeeds.ts`; the staged marble already has its move's seed), so each marble's are its own and go with it when it jumps, through Undo and history too; they flatten towards the edge, shade with the body, and turn with the table in the turned view (`board/marbleSpecks.ts`, one path per shade). The board, the staged marble, the placement preview and every icon draw marbles through it, so a style change lands everywhere. Icons (`MarbleIcon.svelte`) are specked as the board's marbles and each has its own: a tray slot, the pool and the game-end line seed theirs from where they are drawn (`textSeed`), and each marble in a history line has the specks it had on the board (`historyEntries` carries the seeds). Rerun `scripts/render-looks.mjs` after changing the lighting. Never carries interaction state.
- **Action panel height** (`ActionPanel.svelte`, `components/holdTallest.ts`): the panel keeps the tallest height it has reached at its current width, so a line that wraps for a long name does not move the board when it shrinks back. A new width starts over; a remount (the end panel, a new game) starts over too.

## 4. Shared State Contracts

- **Animation scope**: the board animates only `own-action`, `other-action`, `undo`, `history` and `exploration` transitions; page loads (`initial`), server syncs (`reconciliation`), view switches and edits show the board at rest. Without an action (Undo, state-only history) the animator reads a jump, or a whole capture chain a history jump passes over, from the two boards (`board/inferJump.ts`) and flies it straight from its start to its end (0.18s); a marble the step brings back to a standing ring, one placed stepping forward or one a jump took stepping back, drops in by 0.16s without the wobble; vanished marbles lift and fade (0.18s); every such step ends by 0.2s, which `tests/undo-timing.spec.ts` checks.
- **Stopping a move**: the `BoardAnimator` is the session's `moveInterrupter`. Turning the board to the far side lands the move under way at once (`interrupt`), as its motion is laid out in the view it started in; a switch of view (`beforeNewState` with `view-switch`) lands it and skips the rest of its replay (`abandon`). Both finish the context's own timeline with `progress(1)`, never GSAP's global one. A move's board never overwrites a state shown since it began.
- **Marble specks**: each marble carries its seed in the state (`Ring.marbleSeed`), set when it is placed (`placementSeed(coords, actionIndex)`, which the staged marble and the hover preview use too) and moved with it by a jump; a capture's metadata records the jumper's and the captured marble's seeds, and a claim's rings carry theirs, for the history lines. The board, the flier, a lifted or restaged marble and the history read the seed from the ring or the action, so a marble keeps its specks however much history is loaded and through every frame of a move (`tests/specks.spec.ts`).
- **Marbles without seeds**: a marble placed before the state kept seeds, or set out without one in a scenario, is seeded by its ring (`marbleSeed` in `board/marbleSeeds.ts`), so its specks change as it jumps. Games begun before seeds were kept show their old marbles this way.
- **Moving SVG**: the board is drawn at view scale, under a CSS zoom, and Firefox 140 leaves a `matrix()` transform's offset out of that zoom. GSAP writes `x`, `y` and `scale` as `matrix()`, so the animators tween a plain pose per node (`animators/svgPose.ts`) that writes `translate()` and `scale()` itself; GSAP tweens only opacity on the board's nodes. `tests/motion.spec.ts` checks that no frame of a placement, removal, jump, Undo or history step draws a `matrix()`.

- **Staged selection** (`selection`: `color`, `place`, `jumper`): manual choices only, registered with the shared `localSelections`, so the shared Undo pops the latest stage and the header's Undo shows while `hasLocalSelection`. Produced by `chooseColor`, `chooseRing` (a vacant ring stages `place`), `changeStagedColor` and `chooseJumper`; consumed by the session's derived `chosenColor`, `chosenPlace` and `chosenJumper`. Single-option choices are derived, never stored, so Undo skips them. It belongs to the player who made it: anyone else viewing (the harness's Non-active view after an Undo hands it over) sees none of it, and Undo passes it by. The base clears it for every presented transition, except that this session's own Undo of a move with a removal restages its marble (`restagedMove`, matched by the action's index, never in the Non-active view; `restagedZertzSelection`), its colour too when there was a choice. Taps on choices do nothing while a committed move plays (`updatingVisibleState`), as its motion runs on the state it started from. A tap on the board that misses every `.interactive` ring or marble, an unremovable ring included, takes a staged marble off its ring through `letGo` (on a `role="presentation"` wrapper round the board; Undo is the keyboard's way), or else lets go of a chosen colour or jumper.
- **One move**: placing and removing a ring is one `PlaceMarble` action, sent when the ring to remove is chosen. A placement that leaves no ring to remove, or ends the game, is sent as soon as its ring is chosen (`removableAfterPlacing` from the logic package decides). Choosing the staged marble again, or another pool colour, changes its colour in place.
- **Target lists**: derived from the displayed state; empty when the viewer cannot act, including in History View.

## 5. Forbidden Couplings

- Do not derive legality in components; read the session target lists.
- Do not store auto-selected colours or jumpers in `selection`.
- Do not let hover state change anything but the hovered ring's mark and the placement preview.

## 6. Interaction Matrix

| Intent A       | Intent B      | Can coexist? | Winner / combination rule                                                    |
| -------------- | ------------- | ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| remove_ring    | place_marble  | no           | Staging a marble replaces the placement targets with the removal targets     |
| choose_jumper  | place_marble  | no           | A required capture disables placement                                        |
| choose_landing | choose_jumper | yes          | Landing rings are vacant and jumpers are occupied, so no ring has both roles |

## 7. Regression Checklist

- Placement: choosing a colour highlights vacant rings; hover shows the preview; choosing a ring stages the marble there (it drops in) and sends nothing; Undo takes it off, then clears a manual colour.
- Removal: only rings free once the staged marble is placed are marked; choosing one sends the whole move.
- Staged colour: tapping the staged marble, or another pool chip, changes its colour in place.
- Undo of a move: the board returns and the marble stands staged in its ring again, without dropping. Another player's Undo or a step back lifts the marble out instead, and the rings slide back the way they went, all within 0.2s.
- Far side or a switch of view mid-move: the move lands at once.
- Capture: jumpers marked; choosing one shows its landings and fades the other jumpers; a chain fixes the jumper.
- Tap-off: a tap on the board off every target takes a staged marble off its ring, or lets go of a chosen colour or jumper; tapping another jumper switches.
- History View: no targets or pool interaction; opening it removes a staged marble without a lift-off, and the tray's set highlight changes without its transition.
- Animation: a staged marble drops in, and does not drop again when the move goes in; a removed ring slides out through its open sides and fades; a jump flies above other rings, across the table at a steady pace on a round arc as high as 60% of its length, in 0.3s, its shadow shrinking and fading as it rises, and rocks as it lands (0.18s) while the marble it took lifts out (0.3s), so the next jump of a chain can be chosen about 0.5s after the last; reloading or opening a game shows no motion.

## 8. Change Rules

When touching interactive visuals:

1. identify the intent being changed
2. identify the primitives involved
3. verify whether shared state contracts are being widened
4. update this document if a new interaction or coupling is introduced
