# UI Interaction Visual Contract

## 1. Visual Intents

| Intent        | Trigger                                                        | What should be emphasized                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | What should be dimmed                | What must remain unaffected |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| place_piece   | The viewer is setting the board (Take turns, I set, you start) | The chosen colour among the action panel's colour buttons; on the board nothing, since every empty point takes a piece (an invisible tap target on each)                                                                                        | Colours with none left               | Pieces on the board         |
| claim_color   | The viewer may claim (one claim per turn, two total)           | Claimable tokens in the color strip (a cyan ring lying on the ground round each)                                                                                                                                                                | Nothing                              | Board                       |
| choose_stack  | The viewer may move                                            | Every stack with a legal move (thin cyan ring on its top face)                                                                                                                                                                                  | Nothing                              | Color strip                 |
| choose_target | A stack is chosen                                              | The chosen stack (thicker ring); every landing it can reach, directly or through LYNGK-points (dashed ring and spot); each LYNGK-point it can pass through (cyan spot), joined to the chosen stack and each other by flickering lightning bolts | Other movable stacks' rings (hidden) | Color strip                 |

## 2. Visual Primitives

- **Stack** (`StackShape.svelte`, layout in `board/pieceGeometry.ts`, colors and specks in `board/pieceStyle.ts`): discs seen at an angle (elliptical faces, `TILT`), each showing a thick band of its side so every color in the stack reads; the top face shows the moulded rim and a groove with rounded lips and floor, drawn as MATRX GIPF's discs are (`board/pieceShape.ts`). Each color's disc is a pre-rendered WebP (`scripts/render-looks.mjs` from the `/looks` route, `board/lookUrls.ts`); rerun it after changing the shape or a color. A colored piece's specks are drawn over its image per piece, so no two pieces match, as one path per colour (`board/specks.ts`); each joker's are drawn into its own image (`joker-N`, and `joker-N-far` for the far side), picked by its number (pieces are numbered color by color, jokers last: `FIRST_JOKER_ID`, `jokerNumber`), so the three jokers cost no more to draw than any piece. Specks are clipped to the disc's outline (PieceDefs, per radius) so none ever leaves the piece. On the top each lies wholly on the flat or wholly in the groove, running onto the rounded rim, shrinking to keep off the lips and the edge (left out below 0.4 of its size), takes the groove's light, and is left out where the near lip hides it (`topSpeck`); side specks lie evenly round the whole front half and nearly its full height, and take the side's light. Every piece's specks are spread by best-candidate sampling, so they show no bare patches or clumps; a piece's speck paths are worked out once and kept (`board/specks.ts`), so moves do not rebuild them. Stacks are drawn back to front. The bottom disc stays on its point as the stack grows. Flat matte color, no sheen. A stack is drawn from its pieces' ids, bottom to top: each piece's colour follows from its id (`pieceColor`) and its specks are seeded by it (`pieceSeed`), so a piece looks the same wherever it moves, in flight included. Jokers are true white and mottled far more densely than any other piece (640 flecks of black and grey at 0.6 of the usual size), colored pieces lightly flecked (35 specks at 0.9 of the usual size, black 32 with white), spread the same way. Icons of colours with no pieces meant (claims, history) draw each colour's own pieces in turn (`piecesForColors`). Looks are rendered at 6.25 px per board unit, the board's largest fit at 2.5 device pixels per CSS pixel. Never carries interaction state.
- **Stack mark** (`Board.svelte`, mark layer above the stacks): an elliptical ring on the top face's rim (0.89 of the radius, outside the groove), so marks never reach a neighbour. Two-tone, a dark band under a bright core (`--lyngk-mark`, the same bright cyan as the LYNGK-point spots), so it reads on every disc color. Solid for movable stacks (hidden once a stack is chosen; they stay tappable), thicker for the chosen stack, dashed with a centre spot for landings (thick cyan dashes, each outlined in black on every color). A LYNGK-point gets only a cyan spot (`--lyngk-link-mark`); it stays tappable as a movable stack. Inputs `session.movable`, `session.chosenFrom`, `session.landings`, `session.links`.
- **Chain bolts** (`Board.svelte`, `.bolt`, shapes from `board/marks.ts` and `board/lightning.ts`, links from `HydratedLyngkBoard.lyngkConnections`): a jagged bolt between the top faces of each link a LYNGK chain from the chosen stack can make, whether or not a move ends beyond it; a wide translucent halo (a plain stroke, no filter), a cyan body and a white core. Each bolt steps through six seeded shapes in place (SMIL `animate` on `d`, 1/3.6 s per shape, static under reduced motion), so it flickers without ever vanishing; CSS opacity swaps blinked, so SMIL it is. Drawn above every stack. Each path fades with the move on its own, so a bolt's layers never show through each other. Input `session.chainLinks`.
- **Color strip** (`ColorStrip.svelte`, below the action panel at constant height): the first player's claims on the left, neutral colors in the middle, the second player's claims on the right. Neutral tokens the viewer may claim are buttons with a cyan (`--lyngk-mark`) outline. The second player's side is mirrored, so a new claim lands outermost and nothing shifts. On a phone the strip stays one row, as short as the neutral colors allow; a long name gives way. Input `session.claimable`, the claims from `ClaimAnimator.claims`.
- **Board theme** (`app.css`, lines in `board/boardLines.ts`): one warm palette, the printed board's taupe cream table (`--lyngk-table`, `--lyngk-table-deep`), a white play area ending at the grid's outer lines (`--lyngk-play`), black lines with no marks at the nodes, each running a quarter step past its last points.
- **Coordinate labels** (`board/coordinates.ts`): columns A–I, each labelled with its bottom and top point; the single-point side tips (A1, I1) once, below them on either side of the board. Centred on the alphabetic baseline by `dy` (0.35 of the font size) with letter-spacing in px, not `dominant-baseline` or em, which phone browsers draw differently under zoom. They can be hidden (see Board toggles).
- **Board toggles** (`OverlayToggle.svelte`, in `GameTable.svelte`'s overlay): a row of round chips at the board's top right: A1 for the coordinate labels, then, last as in every GIPF-project title, two turning arrows for the far side, the board seen half a turn round (`viewOf(true)`), so what tall stacks hide comes in front; labels stay beyond their columns' ends and the specks on each top turn half round with the board. Each is the player's own title preference (`showCoordinates`, `turned`), saved for the player; coordinates are on by default, the far side off. Each chip's hover text says what a press does now.
- **Action panel height** (`ActionPanel.svelte`, `components/holdTallest.ts`): the panel keeps the tallest height it has reached at its current width, so a line that wraps for a long name does not move the board when it shrinks back. A new width starts over; a remount (the end panel, a new game) starts over too.

## 4. Shared State Contracts

- **Animation scope**: the board and the strip animate only `own-action`, `other-action`, `undo`, `history` and `exploration` transitions; page loads, server syncs, edits and view switches show them at rest. Both play on the step's own timeline, so the presenter waits for them.
- **Reading a step**: every step reads the two boards piece by piece (`boardDiff`): the pieces that leave a point together for another are one run, flown as one flier. The action, or for a step back the move it takes back (`session.undoneMove`, the action whose index is the state's action count), gives a run its LYNGK-points, retraced in reverse on the way back; any other run (a history jump over several moves) flies straight to where it ends.
- **A step with an action** plays in full, history replay included: a placed piece drops in as in GIPF: it falls 50 units over 0.26 s from 1.08 times its size, fading in over the first quarter, with its shadow growing from 0.55, then bounces 4 and 1.5 units over 0.06 s each way; each hop arcs 24 units over 0.32 s (0.24 s per hop of a chain), hovering 8 units over each LYNGK-point, and the landing rebounds, mostly up and down with a little carry along the last hop. A stack the move completes and scores is drawn whole on its point once the landing has settled, then lifts 34 units and fades as one; it reaches its player's card as it lifts, never before. A claim flies from the neutral pieces to its player's side over 0.62 s (lift, arc, touch down short, skip, slide in) while the others slide to make room.
- **A step without an action** (Undo, another player's Undo, a state-only history step) ends within 0.18 s, under the 0.2 s budget: a run's hops share it, with no rebound; a scored stack lifts in the last 0.08 s; a stack put back drops in over the first 0.08 s, with the run's pieces on top, and leaves its card as it starts; a stack gone with no run lifts away and one come with no run drops in. A claim taken back flies home in the same 0.18 s.
- **A lone flier** passes behind the stacks nearer the viewer than its place on the board (`<use>` copies of those stacks drawn over it); with several fliers none is occluded.
- **Interruption**: turning the board lands the move at once, its motion having been laid out in the old view; a switch of view lands it and drops the rest of its replay (`moveInterrupters`). A move's board is written only while its state is still the one shown. All motion is `translate()`/`scale()` through `animators/svgPose.ts`, never GSAP's `matrix()`, which Firefox 140 misplaces under the board's zoom.
- **Staged selection** (`selection`: `from`, a point key): manual choices only, registered with `localSelections`, so Undo lets go of the chosen stack before it undoes the last committed action. The route through LYNGK-points never changes the outcome, so a move is one tap on its destination. A tap on the board that hits nothing choosable (`session.letGo()`, from a `role="presentation"` wrapper round the board) also lets go of it. The selection belongs to the player who made it: anyone else viewing (the harness's Non-active view) sees none of it. Taps do nothing while a move plays. Your own Undo of a move picks its stack up again when it had more than one place to land, checked on the state being shown (`restagedMove`: only the move whose index is that state's action count, and only while the view stays with its maker); with only one place, nothing is held.
- **Target lists**: derived from the displayed state; empty when the viewer cannot act, including in History View.

## 5. Forbidden Couplings

- Do not derive legality in components; read the session lists.

## 6. Interaction Matrix

| Intent A     | Intent B      | Can coexist? | Winner / combination rule                                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | ------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| claim_color  | choose_stack  | yes          | Strip and board are different elements; claiming clears the stack selection                                                                 |
| choose_stack | choose_target | yes          | A tap on a landing lands; a tap on another movable stack (a LYNGK-point included) picks that one up instead; a tap on anything else lets go |

## 7. Regression Checklist

- Choose a stack: rings on every landing it can reach, cyan spots on the LYNGK-points; tap the chosen stack again to put it back, or tap the blank board or a stack with no move to let go.
- Lyngk chain scenario: claim black, choose E7, tap G2 (reached through E5 and G4, which show cyan spots); the stack hops through both, lifts away and scores.
- Undo after a chain: the stack flies back through its LYNGK-points within 0.2 s; no stack left hidden.
- Undo after a scoring move: the scored stack leaves the card and drops back in as the top of it hops home; never on the card and the board at once.
- Undo of a claim, and history steps over one: the claim flies back within 0.2 s.
- Turn the board mid-move: the move lands at once in the new view.
- Undo a move whose stack had several landings: it is held again, its landings marked; Undo again lets go. Undo a move with a single landing: nothing is held.
- History View: no marks or targets.

## 8. Change Rules

When touching interactive visuals:

1. identify the intent being changed
2. identify the primitives involved
3. verify whether shared state contracts are being widened
4. update this document if a new interaction or coupling is introduced
