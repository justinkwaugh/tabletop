# 18xx Game Family

Shared company, ownership, railway map, and tile concepts used across 18xx titles.

## Language

**Company**:
A business represented in the game, with an identity separate from its owners.
The umbrella term for majors, minors, and privates.

**Major**:
A company classified as a major by the title's rules.
Its share structure, capitalization, and operating rules depend on the title.
_Avoid_: Corporation as the name for this category

**Minor**:
A company classified as a minor by the title's rules, typically a smaller operating
railway. Being a minor does not prescribe a particular ownership or share structure.

**Private**:
A company owned as a whole rather than through separately held shares.
Its income, abilities, assets, and permitted owners depend on the title.

**Bank**:
The game's central source and recipient of money, governed by the title's banking rules.

**Owner**:
The player, company, or bank to which an asset belongs.

**President**:
The player or company holding a company's presidency under the title's rules.

**Controlling Owner**:
The player who ultimately controls a company through ownership or presidency,
directly or through other companies. A company may have no controlling owner.
_Avoid_: Controller

**Preprinted Tile**:
Railway content printed directly on a map hex before any tile is placed there.
It belongs to that location and does not enter the tile supply when covered.
_Avoid_: Printed Face, Preprinted Face

**Tile Face**:
The track, stops, revenues, and markings on one side of a tile, whether that tile
is preprinted on the map or is a movable piece from the supply.

**Map Location**:
A named or numbered place on the map, identified independently of its coordinates.
Its preprinted tile and persistent geography remain available when a placed tile
covers it.

**Share**:
A unit of ownership in a company, carrying the rights specified by the title.
_Avoid_: Numbered Right for a PEIR share

**Certificate**:
An individually identifiable ownership instrument representing one or more shares,
or ownership of a private.

**President's Certificate**:
A share certificate that carries the presidency under the title's rules.

**Certificate Limit**:
The maximum permitted total of certificates counted under the title's rules.

**Cash**:
Money owned by a player, company, or bank.

**Treasury**:
The cash and other assets belonging to a company.

**Portfolio**:
The certificates owned by a player or company.

**Certificate Pool**:
A named group of certificates within an owner's holdings, governed by common rules
for their availability or treatment. Pool membership is separate from ownership.
_Avoid_: Holder

**Decision Authority**:
The right to make a particular decision under the title's rules.
It is separate from asset ownership and responsibility for paying an obligation.

**Ownership Limit**:
The maximum permitted shareholding by one owner in a particular company, subject
to the title's exemptions and rules for counting interests.

**Stock Round**:
A round in which participants conduct stock transactions and may form companies
under the title's rules.

**Stock Turn**:
One participant's opportunity to perform the permitted stock actions within a
stock round.

**Stock Market**:
The system of share prices and positions used to track company values under the
title's rules.

**Stock Market Space**:
A distinct position in the stock market, with a price and any rules associated
with occupying that position.

**Stock Market Marker**:
A token identifying a company's current stock-market position.

**Marker Stack**:
The ordered markers sharing one stock-market space, whose relative order may
determine company priority.
