# 18xx Game Family

Shared railway tile concepts used across 18xx titles.

## Language

**Preprinted Tile**:
Railway content printed directly on a map hex before any tile is placed there.
It belongs to that location and does not enter the tile supply when covered.
_Avoid_: Printed Face, Preprinted Face

**Tile Face**:
The track, stops, revenues, and markings on one side of a tile, whether that tile
is preprinted on the map or is a movable piece from the supply.
